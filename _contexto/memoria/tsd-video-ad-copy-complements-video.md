On a video ad, the primary text and headlines are not a caption. The viewer already hears the script; copy that repeats it adds nothing. The copy carries what the video leaves out: the misconception the viewer holds, the fact that makes the want possible (for Concrete Adventures: finished over the slab you already have, no tear-out, about a week), the scene the viewer wants to be in, or the people behind the work. Transcribe the video first (CallRail-style or local faster-whisper) so the copy avoids its lines, then write from the awareness stage (Schwartz) with one fact per paragraph (Ogilvy) and the client's sign-off line.

**Why:** 2026-11-17, Concrete Adventures pool deck video: V1 copy quoted Logan's lines ("Same slab, same afternoon, same sun…"); Edu: "can we do a better copy that doesn't just transcribe what the video says?" V2 (misconception + the want, 4 headlines) replaced it the same hour.

**How to apply:** before building any TSD video ad, get the transcript, list what the video already says, and write texts that complement it; run 2 texts + up to 4 headlines as variants when Edu asks for variants; keep claims to the site and the packet; flag anything (like crew equity numbers) that needs client OK.
