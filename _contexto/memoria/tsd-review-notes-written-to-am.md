The `additional_paid_ads_notes` field on a Client Strategy Review task is read directly by the AM (Angela). Write the asks to her ("Could you check with C&S whether...") and never in the third person ("For Angela to raise: ..."). Only include asks that are new and still open: check the AM's open Internal Requests first and drop anything she already has, plus anything the transcripts show is already handled (e.g. a lead the office marked Booked, a callback already in progress).

**Why:** Edu, 2026-11-13, on the C&S review: "why 'For Angela'? this is going directly to her. It sounds like humans are not even reviewing the text." The bullet also repeated 2 asks from his 11-12 AM note and a lead already marked Booked.

**How to apply:** last bullet = short, addressed to the AM, 1-3 genuinely open items. Related: [[tsd-at-client-strategy-review]], [[tsd-client-facing-voice]], [[tsd-lsa-rating-is-ours]].
