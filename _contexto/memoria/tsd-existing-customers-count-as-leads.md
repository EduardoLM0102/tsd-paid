Existing customers who reach the business through an ad (brand search, LSA, ad call button, ad landing page) **count as qualified leads** in strategy-review KPI rows, weekly passes and call-wins tallies. Say in the notes that they were existing customers, but the ad still caught them, so they stay in the number.

Still NOT counted: job seekers, vendors/solicitors, wrong company, spam, hang-ups / greeting-only / empty voicemails, people outside the service the client offers.

**Why:** Edu, 2026-11-12, while reviewing Spencer: the ad captured the call either way (otherwise a competitor's ad might have), so excluding existing customers understates what paid delivers.

**How to apply:** qualified_leads = new leads + existing customers (show the split, e.g. "33 = 20 new + 13 existing customers"). Supersedes the 2026-11-11 Proud Pops practice of setting existing-customer visits aside and the 11-12 Lilypad/King's first versions.
