TopServ (TSD) own lead-gen Meta ads target home service owners (HVAC/plumbing/electrical/roofing) doing $1M+/yr, offer = "book a call." Videos are VSLs; the primary text + headline just wrap them. Schwartz read = product-aware prospect, stage-4 market → lead with the mechanism (Brandformance / video-as-infrastructure), qualify hard on revenue + spend, let video carry the story.

**Why:** Edu corrected the same mistakes twice; these are his locked rules.

**How to apply:**
- Deliver **2 Primary Texts + 2 Headlines TOTAL for the whole video batch, NOT one set per video** → copy stays generic to the offer/mechanism, no client names or per-video story details.
- Two sizes: Primary Text 1 = smaller, Primary Text 2 = medium. Keep both TIGHT — his "medium" is short; if it looks long it's too long.
- **First line (hook) = 9 words MAX** so it's fully visible on mobile before "See more."
- KEEP paragraph spacing (blank line between paragraphs).
- A couple of emojis, ~1 per paragraph.
- Headlines short + punchy.
- Voice = TSD client-facing (see [[tsd-client-facing-voice]]): owner-to-owner, no em dashes, no "not just X it's Y", no empty adjectives.

Methodology + structure per [[tsd-meta-ads-methodology]]. Copy log with the actual delivered sets lives in topserv-workspace/paid/own-ads/meta-ad-copy-log.md.
