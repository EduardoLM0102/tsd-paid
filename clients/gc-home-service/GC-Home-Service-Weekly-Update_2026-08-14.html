Weekly Campaign Update

Week of August 8 to August 14, 2026

Channels: Google Ads, Meta Ads, and YouTube

Prepared by TopServ Digital

This week's work centered on tightening up lead tracking and landing page consistency on Google, and looking ahead at how to respond to the current stretch of mild, muggy weather with the right offers.

1. What Changed

Google Ads

  • Adjusted the conversion tracking configuration so qualified lead-form submissions are fully reflected in the account's optimization signal going forward.
  • Redirected Performance Max traffic to the dedicated heating & cooling landing page, so every campaign in the account now points to the same fully-tracked, purpose-built page.
  • The budget increase approved last week ($80/day to $242/day combined) is now fully reflected in spend.

Meta Ads

  • No changes this week, awareness campaign running as-is.

2. Why It Changed

  • The tracking adjustment is part of routine account optimization as spend scales up, ensuring lead activity is fully reflected in the data that drives bidding.
  • The landing page consolidation brings Performance Max in line with the rest of the account for consistent measurement.

3. Expected Impact

  • Cleaner lead attribution going forward, supporting better-informed bidding as campaigns learn.
  • Performance Max, the largest budget line, is now fully measurable, which will inform its budget allocation in the coming weeks.

4. Next Steps

  • Humidity & dehumidifier-focused campaigns (Google): with the recent stretch of warm, muggy weather rather than typical summer heat, we believe messaging and search campaigns built around humidity control could generate strong results right now - local search interest in whole-house dehumidifiers is up nearly 4x since April, a strong signal this angle would resonate.
  • Storm/hail-response offer (Meta): it seems the Hamilton/Clermont County area had a hail storm in the last few days - we could capitalize on it with a short-flight offer inviting affected homeowners to get a free outdoor AC unit inspection, geo-targeted to the counties actually affected. This could drive quick, relevant leads.
  • Replacement and tune-up offers could also work well as supporting angles across both platforms.
