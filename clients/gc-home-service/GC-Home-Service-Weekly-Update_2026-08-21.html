Weekly Campaign Update

Week of August 15 to August 21, 2026

Channels: Google Ads, Meta Ads, and YouTube

Prepared by TopServ Digital

This week's headline was a new Meta lead-generation campaign launch, alongside continued gains on Google from last week's tracking and landing page work.

1. What Changed

Meta Ads

  • Launched a new lead-generation campaign built around a Yeti-branded giveaway offer — a CBO structure with seven new static ad creatives, running to a broad audience across the full service area.
  • Retired the prior creative test now that it had run its course, so the account's Meta lead-gen budget is fully behind the new offer.

Google Ads

  • Performance Max ran its first full week pointed entirely at the dedicated heating & cooling landing page, alongside Search and video — every live campaign now shares the same fully-tracked destination.
  • Click volume grew significantly while cost per click came down, meaning the account reached considerably more local searchers for a comparable spend.

2. Why It Changed

  • A giveaway-style offer lowers the barrier to that first lead, a proven way to grow the local database while the more seasonal offers below are still in the works.
  • With Performance Max, Search, and video all pointing to the same purpose-built page, the account is reaching more local searchers for a comparable spend — cost per click fell from about $0.90 to about $0.61 week over week.
  • Phone-tap activity directly from the ads picked up meaningfully alongside the added volume, a good early signal that the extra reach includes real local intent.

3. Expected Impact

  • An early read on lead volume and cost per lead from the Yeti giveaway campaign as it clears the platform's initial delivery ramp-up over the next several days.
  • Broader qualified reach at a lower cost per click on Google, with cleaner attribution now that every campaign shares one tracked destination.

4. Next Steps

  • Monitor the Yeti giveaway campaign (Meta): track early delivery and lead cost as it clears the platform's learning period.
  • Humidity & dehumidifier-focused campaign (Google): still a strong opportunity given the recent stretch of warm, muggy weather — local search interest in whole-house dehumidifiers is up nearly 4x since April, a clear signal this angle would resonate.
  • Storm/hail-response offer (Meta): the recent Hamilton/Clermont County hail event remains a timely, tightly geo-targeted angle worth testing with a short-flight offer inviting affected homeowners to get a free outdoor AC unit inspection.
  • Bringing the video campaign onto the same tracked landing page as Search and Performance Max, for full consistency across the account.
