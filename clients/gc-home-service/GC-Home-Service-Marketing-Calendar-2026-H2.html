The Season This Is Built Around

Cincinnati tri-state HVAC demand runs on a predictable clock: cooling carries through Labor Day, September–October is the fall maintenance/tune-up window, and the first hard cold snap (usually around Thanksgiving) flips demand to no-heat emergencies through the coldest months. Every phase below is sequenced against that, not against whichever channel had a slow week.

Weather-Triggered Campaigns — Mechanism

Cincinnati weather is the best leading indicator this account has: a run of 90°+ days in cooling season or a hard cold snap in heating season moves call volume before search volume catches up. Mechanism: define trigger thresholds per season (e.g. sustained 90°+ for 2+ days May–Sept for AC emergency; ≤20°F or a cold-snap/freeze advisory Nov–Mar for no-heat emergency), and when a public forecast feed (NWS or a free-tier API like OpenWeatherMap — no vendor research done, small-account scale, no enterprise weather-ad platform needed) crosses them, temporarily lift Search budget and pin a weather-referencing RSA headline, mirrored with a short Meta budget lift on the live lead campaign. Build as a Google Ads Script on a schedule, reviewed by hand before each seasonal window rather than fully automatic from day one. Deliberately sequenced behind the ServiceTitan/offline conversion fix — see the Jul 16 PMax lesson above. Fast reaction on dirty signal just buys faster mistakes.

  3. New-mover mailer. Ongoing monthly drop to recent movers into the service area — standard, well-proven HVAC direct-mail trigger.

All three carry a dedicated phone number or promo code for attribution — don't launch a channel that inherits the same measurement gap Google and Meta are currently working through.
