Weekly Campaign Update

Week of August 1 to August 7, 2026

Channels: Google Ads, Meta Ads, and YouTube

Prepared by TopServ Digital

This week's work centered on two builds: getting your search traffic onto a dedicated landing page, and starting the recall push that sets up your mailer drop.

1. What Changed

Landing Page (Google)

  • Your branded search ad now points to a dedicated heating & cooling page instead of the general site homepage. Live since Aug 5, built to match what people are actually searching for.
  • This is the structural piece behind better ad quality and lower cost per click on Google going forward.

Awareness Build, Ahead of the Mailer Drop (Meta + YouTube)

  • Started building recall reach last week specifically to have frequency in place before your mailers land. A broader ZIP-code awareness ad set went live on Meta Aug 6, and a new YouTube awareness campaign, also ZIP-code targeted, went live Aug 7, same goal, second channel.
  • The idea: people who have already seen you across Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube respond better to a mailer in their hand, and search you by name when it lands.
  • Early read on the Meta ad set is strong: $0.39 per person who recalls seeing you in its first two days. YouTube just started Friday, so it's one day of data in this report, too early to read, but it's live and running.

A Note on Conversion Counts This Week

  • Google is still settling in from the July 28 change, when we raised the bar on what counts as a lead there, from a 15 second call to a full minute call or a form. Changes like that typically take one to two weeks to fully recalibrate, so signal is thinner than usual right now. Combined with the new landing page going live the same week, the account is mid-upgrade on two fronts at once. Both are expected to settle out, not a concern.

Also Running

  • Hiring campaign continued for the Journeyman and Lead Installer roles, 6 more applications this week.

3. Expected Impact

Everything above is in its first days of running. Expect these numbers to keep moving over the next several days as each change fully kicks in.

  • Landing page. Sets up lower cost per click and better ad quality on Google once volume builds, including the volume the mailers are about to send toward branded search. Google is still recalibrating around the new page and the new conversion goals, so give it the next few days to settle before reading it as a trend.
  • Awareness ahead of the mail drop. Reach across three channels now means more people who already recognize you when the mailer lands in the next couple of weeks. That's the multi-touch effect: mail plus prior recall beats mail alone. Cost per recall should keep improving as these campaigns build more data over the coming days.

A note on budget. Spend is intentionally higher right now to properly fund the landing page rollout and the recall push ahead of your mailer drop. Once that investment period has run its course, budget steps back down toward the previously planned level.
