All Seasons Air Conditioning

Proposed Monthly Ad Budget

Budgets and results as of October 16, 2026.

Prepared by Gui, Senior Media Buyer · Approved by Eduardo, Head of Paid Ads

We're proposing three monthly budgets for the heating season: $4,630 at minimum, $7,400 halfway, and $10,150 at the most we can put to good use. All three keep Local Services Ads on every day of the month, and Facebook and Instagram stay off.

Local Services Ads brings in leads for about $66 each, but it tops out around $2,500 a month. Past that, more money mostly raises the price per lead. So most of the growth goes to Google search, which we tightened on October 16, and to the Mitsubishi co-op campaign, where you pay about a third of the cost. Heating searches on Long Island nearly double in November, so this is when it pays to be showing up.

Local Services Ads, $2,000 to $2,500. In the first half of October it spent $922 and brought in 14 leads before hitting its monthly limit, so $2,000 keeps it running the whole month. We stop at $2,500 because past that point Google mostly charges more per lead instead of sending more of them. When it went from about $300 a month in July to October's pace of about $1,850, leads only went from 18 to about 28 a month.

Google heating and cooling searches, $1,500 to $5,500. This is where a bigger investment can actually go to work. Before the budget cut, these campaigns were missing 44% to 84% of the searches they qualified for because the daily budget ran out. Now that searches for other companies are blocked, more of every dollar goes to people looking for heating and AC work in your area. $1,500 is the least that still brings in enough calls to judge Google within a few weeks. $5,500 is about as far as we'd go before the extra money starts buying weaker searches.

Mitsubishi ductless co-op, $730 to $1,750. Mitsubishi and Homans Associates each cover a third of approved spend, so you pay about a third of this line once the claims are paid. About $3,950 is left in the approved co-op plan through December, and the maximum uses it up across November and December. Preapprovals are due November 15, and 2026 claims are due January 4, 2027.

All Seasons name searches come back on at about $13 a day, so competitors can't pick off people searching for you by name during the busy months. Facebook and Instagram stay off in all three options. If you choose the maximum, we'd raise Google in two steps over two weeks so its bidding has time to adjust.

4. What to Expect

At $2,000 a month, Local Services Ads should bring in roughly 28 calls and messages a month, going by its pace in early October. At $2,500, expect around 31, at a higher price per lead. On Google, the extra budget mainly means showing up for more of the heating and AC searches in your area that we're missing today.

Last year, heating searches nearly doubled from October to November and stayed high through February, outnumbering AC searches from November through March. Most Local Services leads since October 9 have been heating jobs.
