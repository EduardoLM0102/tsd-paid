6. What We Worked On

Most of this went live on October 7 and 8, so the effect will show up in the next report.

  • Lead tracking. Job applications from the careers page are now their own event, so the lead count shows customers only. Performance Max no longer sends visitors to the careers page, and job searches like "jobs near me" are excluded across the account.
  • Electrical Search. Ads now land on the electrical services page. Google's AI Max feature is off, so the ads match the searches and pages we choose, and 39 unrelated searches were excluded after a search term review.
  • Plumbing and HVAC Search. Excluded competitor name searches on both. On Plumbing we also set a cost-per-click ceiling, added a general plumber ad group, and pointed the ads at the plumbing and water heater pages.
  • Branded Search. Raised the daily budget from $70 to $100 by moving $30 from Performance Max HVAC, so total budget didn't change. Added "Pops Electric" searches and widened it to your full 47-city service area, New Braunfels included.
  • Location targeting. HVAC and Plumbing now reach the 20-city San Antonio metro instead of the city alone, and ads only show to people who are actually in the area.
  • Performance Max. Searches for "Proud Pops" now go to the Branded campaign.
  • Ads and links. Wrote 14 new ads that match what's on your website (in Google review now) and added a "Book a Free Pop-In" link to the schedule page on all Search ads.
  • Calls. Google now counts a call from an ad only when it lasts at least 60 seconds.
