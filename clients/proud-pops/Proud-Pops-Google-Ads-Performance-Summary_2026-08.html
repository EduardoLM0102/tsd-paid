5. What We Worked On

  • Built a dedicated landing page for HVAC search traffic and pointed the AC repair, installation, and maintenance ads there instead of the general homepage, so that traffic lands somewhere built specifically for the offer.
  • Increased investment behind the HVAC campaigns, Search and Performance Max both, heading into peak cooling season, and set performance targets to guide Google's bidding toward efficient volume.
  • Sharpened HVAC targeting with additional keyword refinements to keep spend focused on the right searches.
  • Increased investment behind the Branded and Electrical Search campaigns after finding both were positioned to capture meaningfully more visibility for the added budget.
  • Refined the ad messaging inside the HVAC Performance Max campaign, tightening a handful of assets that were carrying mixed messaging so the campaign speaks more directly to HVAC customers.
  • Tuned network settings on the Plumbing Search campaign to keep delivery focused on Google Search.
  • Launched two new YouTube brand-awareness campaigns, bringing Proud Pops' video content to homeowners in San Antonio and New Braunfels.
  • Streamlined how leads are counted in the account, removing a secondary click-based signal from the tally so lead reporting reflects genuine customer contacts more precisely going forward.

6. What's Next

  • Extend the dedicated landing page approach used for HVAC to the Electrical and Plumbing campaigns.
  • Keep building out call tracking so phone activity can be counted alongside form leads with full confidence.
  • Carry the HVAC investment through the rest of the cooling season.
  • Watch the new YouTube campaigns as they clear their initial delivery ramp.
