What we optimized, and what's nextOctober 16 to November 10, 2026

What we optimized

  • Budget plan. Brand and Local Services only through November 3, then Electrical, HVAC and Plumbing back on November 4 with daily budgets set to land November on plan. Performance Max and YouTube stay paused for now.
  • Call tracking connected to Google Ads. Confirmed calls (first-time callers, over a minute) now report back to Google, the ads' call button uses the tracked number 210-225-5767, and Google optimizes toward confirmed calls and customer forms. Taps and repeat callers are tracked separately.
  • Branded Search. Moved to a top-of-page impression strategy with two underperforming ads retired. Brand clicks dropped from $12 to $18 to about $4, so the daily budget moved from $130 to $50 and the difference went to Electrical.
  • Search refinements. Competitor and off-service searches excluded, the broad "electrical services" keyword retired in favor of the specific versions, general plumber searches routed to the general plumbing ad group, and a $30 cost-per-click ceiling on HVAC.
  • Call review. Every recorded call and Local Services message from the period was reviewed and logged. This is now part of every review, so the lead counts you see are verified.
