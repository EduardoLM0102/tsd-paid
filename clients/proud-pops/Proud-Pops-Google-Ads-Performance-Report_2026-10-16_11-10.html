6. What We Worked On

  • Budget plan. With the move to $25,000 a month, we ran Branded Search and Local Services only through November 3 so October landed close to the new level, then brought Electrical, HVAC and Plumbing Search back on November 4 with daily budgets set to land November on plan. Performance Max and YouTube stay paused for now.
  • Call tracking connected to Google Ads. CallRail now reports confirmed calls (first-time callers, over a minute) back to Google Ads, the ads' call button uses the tracked number 210-225-5767, and Google optimizes toward confirmed calls and customer forms. Taps and repeat callers are kept as separate signals.
  • Branded Search. Moved to a top-of-page impression share strategy for Proud Pops searches, with two underperforming ads retired. Brand clicks now cost a fraction of what they did, so the daily budget moved from $130 to $50 and the difference went to Electrical ($375 to $455).
  • Search refinements. 40 competitor and off-service terms excluded account-wide on October 16, and 16 more competitor names on November 9. On Electrical, the broad "electrical services" keyword was retired in favor of the specific versions. On Plumbing, general plumber searches now go to the general plumbing ad group instead of the leak detection one. On HVAC, a $30 cost-per-click ceiling keeps the most competitive searches in check.
  • Site links. The "Our Story" and "Contact Us" links on the ads now point to the new proudpops.com pages.
  • Call review. Listened to all 39 recorded calls and read the 7 Local Services message threads from the period, logged the outcome of each one, and rebuilt the lead count on what the callers actually asked for. This is now part of every review.
  • Account cleanup. The leftover Smart campaign in the My Favorite Service Company account was paused on November 9 so all investment runs through the Proud Pops campaigns.

7. Budget and Pacing

November 1 to 10 came to $5,715.68 across Search and Local Services. At the current pace, November lands around $20,600 against the $25,000 plan. Electrical now shows for every search its budget allows, Plumbing and HVAC have more headroom in ad rank than in budget, and HVAC runs under a cost-per-click ceiling by design, so adding budget to Search would mostly buy lower-quality clicks.

Recommendation: keep the $25,000 monthly plan. For the roughly $4,000 November difference there are two options: put it into brand awareness on YouTube (about $150 a day on the San Antonio and New Braunfels video campaigns, which are built and ready), or let November come in under plan. We'll take direction on that in the review call.
