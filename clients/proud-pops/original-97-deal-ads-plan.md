- **Disclosure trap (US credit ads):** a monthly payment amount in an ad is a "trigger term" under Truth in Lending (Reg Z): the
  ad also has to state the APR, the term and "with approved credit" (or the down payment). The $165/mo Comfort Plan is a lease,

  must include the fine print for each number, not just the number.

The Original 97 Deal: how we'll run the ads

We'll run the campaign on Google and on Facebook and Instagram, built around three messages. Each one runs as its own set of
ads, so we can see which one brings in the most buyers:

- The price: any size, gas or heat pump, $9,797. A new system at one flat price is something most homeowners have never seen.
- The low monthly payment: a new heating and cooling system as low as $135 a month.
- The Comfort Plan: a new system with maintenance, filters and covered repairs for $165 a month, shown first, as you asked.

A fourth set of ads tells the story behind the deal: you and Jacob, 30 years in the trade since 1997. Short videos of the two
of you will do more for this offer than any graphic, so we'd like to film a few.

On Google, the deal and the monthly payment go into the ads people see when they search for a new AC, furnace or heat pump,
with the deal shown as a promotion through the end date you pick.

Everyone who visits the deal page and doesn't finish will see follow-up ads for the next few weeks, so someone who looked at
the price on Monday is reminded of the monthly payment on Thursday.

How we'll measure it: every step of the page will be tracked, from starting the journey to reserving an install date, booking
a Pop-In or asking for a call. If we can receive your sales from Path, we can report cost per installed system, which is the
number you said matters most.

Once the wording is approved and the deal page is up, the first ads can be live within two days, ahead of Black Friday.
