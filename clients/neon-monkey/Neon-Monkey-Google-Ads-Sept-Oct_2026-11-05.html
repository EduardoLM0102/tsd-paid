A Note on September's Numbers

On August 29, an update to the neonmonkeytree.com website removed the Google tracking code we had installed on the site. The code went back on October 13. Until then, Google couldn't see calls that started on the website or most of the form fills. What it could still count were calls placed straight from the ads and some contact form fills. So the lead counts for September and early October are lower than what really came in, and cost per lead looks higher than it actually was. Tracking has been working normally since October 13.

Leads are form fills plus calls from the ads that lasted at least a minute. Performance Max doesn't use keywords, so it's counted with non-branded. Its clicks cost less because they include YouTube, Maps and display placements, not only search. Local Services ads aren't included here.

What the Numbers Say

  • Performance Max brought in the most leads at the lowest cost, $170 per lead across the two months, and it got cheaper in October ($150, down from $196 in September).
  • Non-branded Search had 16 leads in September and 4 in October. Most of October's spend came before October 19, when we capped what it pays per click and started narrowing the searches it shows for. Since then a click costs under $20, down from $27.78 in September.
  • Branded Search showed for most people searching the Neon Monkey name. Not many people search the name, so it stays a small part of the budget. Since October 19 we cap what we pay for a branded click, which had been running $19 to $25.

What We've Done

  • September 28: Tuned lawn care and landscaping searches out of non-branded Search.
  • October 13: Tracking code back on the website. Form fills and website calls are counting again.
  • October 16: Turned off Google's auto-applied recommendations and its automatic ad text, so nothing changes in the account without us reviewing it first.
  • October 19: Non-branded Search moved to a bid strategy with a $20 limit per click. Branded moved to a set limit per click. Performance Max now only shows to people who are actually in your service area, not people elsewhere searching about it.
  • October 20: Added The Tree Place as a negative keyword on all campaigns.
  • October 26: Tuned out do-it-yourself and rental stump searches (how to, DIY, rental, stump grinder). Performance Max now targets the same 36 West Fort Worth locations as Search, with Dallas, Arlington, Irving and Grand Prairie left out. Raised the branded click limit a little so the ads keep showing for searches of your name.
  • November 2: A call now counts as a lead once it reaches 90 seconds, up from 60. The phone menu takes about 25 seconds, so this means about a minute of real conversation. Performance Max no longer shows for searches about Davey Tree, Asplundh, Oncor, utility tree work or fertilizer.
  • November 4: Tuned searches for other local tree companies (Four Seasons, Wright Tree) out of non-branded Search.

What's Next

  • Week of November 11: We review non-branded Search on its new setup and decide whether its budget stays there or moves to the campaigns booking more jobs.
  • Week of November 16: Same review for Performance Max, now that only calls of 90 seconds or more count as leads.
  • Local Services ads booked jobs at about $78 each over the last two weeks of October, the lowest of any channel, so that's the first place we'd look if budget moves.
  • On your side: 7 of the 18 calls that came from the ads between October 20 and November 3 never got past the phone menu. Adding a default option, so a caller who doesn't press anything goes straight to a person, would keep more of those calls.
