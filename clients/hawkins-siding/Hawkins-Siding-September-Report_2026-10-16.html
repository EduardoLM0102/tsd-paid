Paid Advertising Report

September was a strong month for Google Local Services, the Google Guaranteed listing at the top of search. It brought in 69 leads, and 29 of them were marked as booked jobs, which works out to about $194 per booked job. Across all of your Google advertising, your ads brought in 98 leads for the month.

Total ad spend for September was $8,441.46, all on Google: $5,635.98 on Local Services, $2,409.78 on Search and $395.70 on Performance Max. The Facebook and Instagram campaigns were paused for the month, so there was no spend there.

1. September Highlights

  • 29 booked jobs from Local Services, about 4 out of every 10 Local Services leads.
  • 98 leads across Local Services, Search and Performance Max.
  • 112 first-time callers reached your tracked line, 77 of them through Google Local Services and Google Ads.
  • 20 Local Services leads at no charge. Google only bills for leads it treats as valid.

7. What We Updated

  • New ad groups (end of August, running all September): the main Search campaign was split into four groups (siding replacement, siding repair, vinyl siding and general siding), each with its own ad written around those searches. Keywords that weren’t getting searches were trimmed.
  • Search refinements (October 5): your ads no longer show for handyman searches, Angi, home repair grants, do-it-yourself searches or building supply companies. A small part of the Search budget moved to Performance Max, with the total unchanged.
  • Brand searches (October 14): searches for your business name now run through their own campaign with a set bid, so your ad is the first thing people see when they look you up. The Performance Max budget went from $18 to $23 a day and Search from $75 to $70, keeping the same total.
  • Call quality and more search refinements (October 16): calls from your ads now count once they last a full minute. Your ads no longer show for paint stores, gutter companies, people looking up a siding brand’s phone number, or job searches. The main keywords were also tightened so they match searches for siding work more closely.
