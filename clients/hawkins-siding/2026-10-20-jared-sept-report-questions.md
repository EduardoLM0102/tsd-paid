## 2026-10-22 FINAL client answer (replaces the draft above; for Angela to send Jared)

Status: written 10-22, Edu's cuts applied (JobNimbus, LSA disputes), ready to post in the task's Execution notes.

Here's where each of those numbers comes from, plus what we've changed on Search since September.

**Booked jobs**
The booked count comes from the lead status in the Google Local Services inbox. When a lead gets marked "Booked" there, it shows as booked in the report. There's no JobNimbus connection today, so the number is only as accurate as the marking in that inbox.

**Local Services leads and cost**
Google only billed for 49 of the 69 leads. The other 20 were free (mostly hang ups under 30 seconds), so dividing the spend by 69 made the cost look lower than what you actually paid. On the billed leads, September came out to $115 per lead and $194 per booked job, with 29 jobs booked.

**Google Search conversions**
The 25 in Google Ads are 16 calls plus 9 form fills. In September Google counted any call longer than 10 seconds, and only 8 of those 16 calls lasted a minute or more. The report counts the 8 longer calls plus the 9 forms, which is 17. Siding General, Siding Repair, Siding Replacement and Vinyl Siding are ad groups inside one Search campaign, so the 25 is the total for all four.

**What we found on Search and what we changed**
To see what those calls really were, we listened to the Search call recordings from the first half of October. Most of the callers weren't homeowners looking for siding. They had searched for another siding company, a supply store or a product brand and reached Hawkins by mistake. Only 1 of 16 Search calls became a booked job, while Local Services booked 7 of 10 over the same days. Google was counting those calls as wins and going after more of the same searches.

Between October 14 and 22 we:
- blocked close to 40 searches for other companies, suppliers, stores and job seekers
- set the ads to show only when someone searches for the service itself, like "siding repair" or "vinyl siding"
- capped what we pay for a click at $12 (a few had gone over $30)
- stopped Google from treating every call as a win, so it now aims for real form leads, and we check the Search calls ourselves by listening to the recordings
- got the brand campaign showing again, so people searching "Hawkins Siding" see your ad first, at under $5 a click

Search will get fewer clicks for a bit, but they should be the right ones. We'll review the first full week of the new setup after October 29 and send you the results.

**Budget**
Local Services is your strongest channel, and budget isn't what limits it. In September it had room to spend $357 a day and averaged about $188, because that's how many leads Google had for your area. More budget there wouldn't have bought more leads. In October we moved part of the Search budget ($80 down to $67 a day) into the Google campaign that runs on YouTube and Google's partner sites, which has been bringing in form leads at about $26 each, and total spend stayed the same. If your office can tell us whether those form leads have been real siding jobs, that tells us how far to push it. Once the October 29 numbers are in, we'll compare Search against Local Services and recommend whether more of the budget should move.
