1. How Retargeting Is Set Up

Your panel upgrade ads run in three steps. Step 1 is the first-touch videos, shown to adults 25 to 65 who live within 50 miles of your shop on South Garnett Road. Anyone who watches at least 3 seconds of one of those videos moves into the step 2 pool and starts seeing a different set of videos about what the upgrade itself looks like. Anyone who watches at least 10 seconds of a step 2 video moves on to step 3, which is about trust: a local, family-owned team that has been doing this for ten years. Facebook can also widen steps 2 and 3 to nearby people who behave like those viewers when it expects them to ask for an estimate.

Every step uses Facebook's instant form, so people can ask for a free estimate without leaving the app. Anyone who fills it out is taken out of all three steps for 90 days, so nobody keeps getting ads after they've reached out. People stay in the step 2 and step 3 pools for 120 days after they watch.

5. Google Ads Adjustments on October 20

We made five adjustments to the Google campaigns on October 20. The total daily budget stays the same at $235 a day.

  • Brand ads now run all week, around the clock. They used to run Monday to Friday, 6 AM to 10 PM. Now anyone searching for Watt's Right at night or on the weekend sees your ad at the top.
  • Brand Search budget went from $20 to $35 a day so it can cover all of those searches.
  • Non-Brand Search budget went from $120 to $105 a day, with a target of $80 per lead. This tells Google to bid for leads at that cost instead of paying top dollar for every click.
  • 11 other electrical company names were blocked from showing your non-brand ads, since people searching for a specific company are rarely looking to switch.
  • The emergency electrician ad was updated. We tested a new version that puts the search term and Tulsa in the headline. From October 1 to 19 it was clicked more often (3.56% of views against 2.82%) and turned twice as many clicks into leads (33% against 17%), so the older version was paused.

Early sign: on October 21, the first full day with these changes, your brand ad showed for 78% of searches for your name, against an average of 37% from October 9 to 19. One day is too early to judge the rest of these changes.
