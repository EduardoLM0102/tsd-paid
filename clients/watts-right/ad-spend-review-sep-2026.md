## Recommendation: +37% on Search &amp; Performance Max

We pulled impression share on each paid campaign to separate a **budget** problem (fixable with more money) from a **competitive/rank** problem (isn't). Performance Max and EV Charger are losing most of their missed reach to budget alone. EV Charger is 80% budget-capped, with almost zero rank loss. Non-branded's missed reach splits evenly between budget and rank, so more money there only recovers half the ceiling; Branded isn't budget-constrained at all. New spend goes where it buys the most at today's cost per lead:
