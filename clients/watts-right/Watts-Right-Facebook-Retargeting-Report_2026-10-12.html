This report covers your Facebook and Instagram retargeting ads for panel upgrades, the ads that only show to people who have already watched one of your videos.

1. How Retargeting Is Set Up

Your panel upgrade ads run in three steps. Step 1 is the first-touch videos, shown to adults 25 to 65 who live within 50 miles of your shop on South Garnett Road. Anyone who watches at least 3 seconds of one of those videos moves into the step 2 pool and starts seeing a different set of videos about what the upgrade itself looks like. Anyone who watches at least 10 seconds of a step 2 video moves on to step 3, which is about trust: a local, family-owned team that has been doing this for ten years.

Every step uses Facebook's instant form, so people can ask for a free estimate without leaving the app. Anyone who fills it out is taken out of all three steps for 90 days, so nobody keeps getting ads after they've reached out. People stay in the step 2 and step 3 pools for 120 days after they watch.
