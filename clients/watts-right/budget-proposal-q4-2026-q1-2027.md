## Where the Extra Money Goes

**Google Search.** People searching for Watt's Right by name are your lowest-cost leads on any platform, and today the ad still misses some of those searches when the daily budget runs out, so every option raises that budget first. The rest goes to service searches (emergency electrician, panel upgrades, EV chargers), where most of the booked jobs from ads come from, with room to pay a little more per lead so your ads show on more of them. We'd add a new ad group for whole-home generators and surge protection, timed for winter storms and outages.

**Performance Max.** Stays at today's budget. It brings in a good number of calls, but when we went through the recordings, many of them weren't service requests (callers looking for their utility company or a store, for example). There's a lot of room to improve that, so we're tightening which searches it shows on first, and the extra money goes to Google Search, where calls turn into booked jobs more often. We'll revisit it at the first monthly check.

**Facebook and Instagram.** Most of the extra goes to the campaign that reaches new people around Tulsa, which brings in the lowest-cost Facebook leads today (about $42 each). Options 2 and 3 also raise the brand video budget, so more people see Watt's Right a few times a week and already know the name when something goes wrong in February.

## Month by Month

- **November:** Panel upgrades and whole-home surge protection, before the cold weather puts more load on older panels. The brand video starts building familiarity going into the slower months.
- **December:** Emergency service and breaker restoration, with the 24/7, nights, weekends and holidays message front and center.
- **January and February:** Whole-home generators and 24/7 emergency response during winter storms and outages. February is the month the extra budget matters most, so we'd keep it running the whole month rather than pulling back.
- **March:** Panel upgrades, EV chargers and wiring upgrades as spring projects start back up.

## How We'll Track It

We'll check every platform weekly and send a monthly summary of leads, cost per lead and spend by platform against this plan. We count real calls, messages and form fills, not button taps, and we review the call recordings so we can keep improving lead quality. If one platform starts producing cheaper leads, we'll move money toward it within the approved monthly total.

## Our Recommendation

**Option 2: Grow, starting November 1 and running through March 31.** It adds about 70 leads a month for about $5,500 more (about $165,800 for the five months), keeps the cost per lead in the low $60s, and gives February enough extra budget to fill most of the seasonal dip. If lead volume is still short of what your team can handle after the first month, Option 3 is the next step up.
