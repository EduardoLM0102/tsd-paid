What Winter Looked Like Last Year

Google Guaranteed has run the longest, so it's the best read on how winter demand moves. Last year, calls held at about the same level from October through January. February was the slow month, about 15 to 20% below the rest of the winter, and things picked back up in March.

You're getting about 70 to 90% more Google Guaranteed leads than this time last year, so this winter should start from a higher base with the same pattern: steady through January, a dip in February.

Google Guaranteed charges per lead, and its budget isn't what's holding it back: over the last 30 days it used about a quarter of its cap. More money there wouldn't buy more leads, so the extra budget in each option goes where it does: Google Search, Performance Max, and Facebook and Instagram.

Where the Extra Money Goes

Google Search. More budget for service searches (emergency electrician, panel upgrades, EV chargers) and room to pay a little more per lead, so your ads show on more of those searches. Options 2 and 3 also add budget to your name searches, so your ad shows more often when someone looks up Watt's Right. We'd add a new ad group for whole-home generators and surge protection, timed for winter storms and outages.

Performance Max. A higher daily budget. Today it runs out of budget before it reaches about a quarter of the searches it could show on.

Facebook and Instagram. Most of the extra goes to the campaign that reaches new people around Tulsa, which brings in the lowest-cost Facebook leads today (about $42 each). Options 2 and 3 also raise the brand video budget, so more people see Watt's Right a few times a week and already know the name when something goes wrong in February.

Month by Month

  • November: Panel upgrades and whole-home surge protection, before the cold weather puts more load on older panels. The brand video starts building familiarity going into the slower months.
  • December: Emergency service and breaker restoration, with the 24/7, nights, weekends and holidays message front and center.
  • January and February: Whole-home generators and 24/7 emergency response during winter storms and outages. February is the month the extra budget matters most, so we'd keep it running the whole month rather than pulling back.
  • March: Panel upgrades, EV chargers and wiring upgrades as spring projects start back up.

How We'll Track It

We'll check every platform weekly and send a monthly summary of leads, cost per lead and spend by platform against this plan. We count real calls, messages and form fills, not button taps, so the numbers line up with what your office sees. If one platform starts producing cheaper leads, we'll move money toward it within the approved monthly total.

Our Recommendation

Option 2: Grow, starting November 1 and running through March 31. It adds about 65 leads a month for about $5,500 more (about $165,800 for the five months), keeps the cost per lead in the low $60s, and gives February enough extra budget to fill most of the seasonal dip. If lead volume is still short of what your team can handle after the first month, Option 3 is the next step up.
