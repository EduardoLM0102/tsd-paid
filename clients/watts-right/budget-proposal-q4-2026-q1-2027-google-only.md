## Where the Extra Money Goes

**Google Search.** People searching for Watt's Right by name are your lowest-cost leads on any platform, and today the ad still misses some of those searches when the daily budget runs out, so every option raises that budget first. The rest goes to service searches (emergency electrician, panel upgrades, EV chargers), where most of the booked jobs from ads come from, with room to pay a little more per lead so your ads show on more of them.

Options 2 and 3 also open up the biggest group of searches in your area: people typing "electrician" or "electrician near me" around Tulsa, several thousand searches a month. We'd add those along with a new ad group for whole-home generators and surge protection, timed for winter storms and outages. These general searches cost more per click, which is most of why the cost per lead rises in Options 2 and 3.

Option 3 is about as much as Google Search can put to good use in Tulsa right now. Past that point, more budget mostly raises what we pay per click instead of bringing in more calls.

**Performance Max.** Stays at today's budget. It brings in a good number of calls, but when we went through the recordings, many of them weren't service requests (callers looking for their utility company or a store, for example). There's a lot of room to improve that, so we're tightening which searches it shows on first, and the extra money goes to Google Search, where calls turn into booked jobs more often. We'll revisit it at the first monthly check.

## How We'll Track It

We'll check every platform weekly and send a monthly summary of leads, cost per lead and spend by platform against this plan. We count real calls, messages and form fills, not button taps, and we review the call recordings so we can keep improving lead quality. If one platform starts producing cheaper leads, we'll move money toward it within the approved monthly total.

## Our Recommendation

**Option 2: Grow, starting November 1 and running through March 31.** It adds about 50 leads a month for about $5,500 more (about $127,000 for the five months), keeps the cost per lead around $70, and gives February enough extra budget to fill most of the seasonal dip. If lead volume is still short of what your team can handle after the first month, Option 3 is the next step up.
