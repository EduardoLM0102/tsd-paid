The Season This Is Built Around

Spencer Air's live geo-targeting (Google LSA + Search, Meta lead ad sets) centers on the DFW/North Texas metro (Bedford, Fort Worth, Dallas, Arlington, Euless, Keller, Burleson, Crowley, Wylie, Anna, Grand Prairie, Sanger, Springtown). That market is cooling-dominant: a long, hot cooling season runs roughly April through September/October, and winter is short and mild with only occasional hard-freeze risk (the Feb 2021 Texas grid freeze is the standing reminder that "mild" doesn't mean "zero emergency demand"). Q1 sits in the lowest-demand part of this account's year — no cooling season pressure yet, and heating demand is thin and freeze-driven rather than sustained. That's useful: Q1 is naturally the right quarter to do foundational/gate-clearing work (CAPI restore, audience rebuild, geo-targeting fix) before budget needs to follow the cooling ramp starting in Q2.

Lessons From 2026 Performance

Google (customer 2524618054)

  • LSA is the account's real engine, and it hasn't shown saturation yet. Monthly LSA spend scaled roughly 4.5x from January to August ($1,094.89 → $4,959.65) while cost-per-lead held in a tight $73–110 band the entire time (Jan $109.49, Feb $103.15, Mar $73.02, Apr $90.84, May $100.92, Jun $90.59, Jul $95.93, Aug $88.32 through the 25th) — CPA did not degrade as spend went up. That's the strongest signal in the account for where Q1 budget should go first.
  • Branded Search is budget-capped today. Last 30 days: 79.6% impression share, 74.5% top IS, 59.8% absolute top IS — but 11.3% is lost to budget, not rank, on a $50/day cap that's essentially fully spent ($1,530.12 actual vs. ~$1,500 cap). This is the cheapest lead in the account ($35.85 CPA last 30 days) and doesn't need to wait for Q1 — it's a same-week fix.
  • The July branded-search conversion count needs verification before it's trusted. Conversions went from 22.13 in June to 71.99 in July on essentially flat spend ($1,411.52 → $1,539.41) — CPA fell from $63.79 to $21.38 in one month. Too sharp a jump to take at face value without checking whether a conversion-goal/counting change (not real demand) drove it — this should be reconciled in Q1 before it anchors any budget math.
  • Nonbranded Search had one real trial and it failed. TSD_07.05_Leads_Search_Nonbranded_Cooling spent $712.88 in May for 59 clicks and zero real conversions. It's been PAUSED since. Not a Q1 candidate — no working formula exists for it on this account yet.
  • No PMax exists anywhere in this account's history. Not a Q1 default either — a deliberate yes/no decision for later in the year, not now.
  • LSA lead-level quality detail (disputed/booked/charged) is not accessible via the API — the local_services_lead query returned a permission error specific to that resource. Worth requesting Local Services detailed-lead-reporting access from Google as a Q1 task, since LSA is the account's largest spend line and currently unverifiable on quality.

Meta (account 1845825982372184)

  • Meta was completely dark for five months this year ($0 spend Jan–May), only back online June 30. Everything below is based on roughly 8 weeks of live data — a reason to keep Q1 Meta spend disciplined rather than scaling on a thin track record.
  • Both active lead campaigns leak out-of-area leads. 07/01/26 - CBO | Leads and 08/11/26 - CBO | Leads, both named "Texas Locations," are delivering 22% and 30% of leads respectively to people in Louisiana, Mississippi, Oklahoma, and Oregon. The newer campaign's lower headline cost-per-lead ($50.44 vs. $79.88 in August) is partly an artifact of that leak. This needs fixing in Q1 before any Meta budget increase — check the location-targeting type (living-in vs. broader) and whether Advantage+ Audience expansion is on.
  • No retargeting layer exists. Only three LOOKALIKE audiences, all created 2017-10-04, all INACTIVE, seeded from stale 2017 page-likers. Meanwhile the Awareness campaign has delivered 333,005 impressions this year at a strong $2.40 CPM — reach with nothing capturing it. Q1's low-demand season is the right window to build this before it's needed for the Q2 cooling ramp.
  • Conversions API has been dead since June 2023. Browser pixel is healthy (last fired 2026-08-23) but server-side hasn't fired in over three years. Fixing this is a Q1 technical prerequisite for everything else on the Meta side.

Q1 2027 allocation logic:

  1. Google gets the growth budget first, but modestly. LSA has shown 8 straight months of rising spend with flat-to-improving CPA — recommend a single step-up to the LSA weekly cap for Q1 (roughly +15–20% over the current run-rate) rather than a large jump, and let the response set the pace for Q2.
  2. Recover Branded Search's lost budget share immediately — doesn't need to wait for Q1 to start, but should be locked in as part of the Q1 baseline regardless. Lifting the $50/day cap to close the 11.3% budget-lost impression share is close to risk-free ROI.
  3. Don't fund Nonbranded Search in the Q1 baseline. Gate it on a real negative-keyword build and confirmed landing page, revisit later in the year.
  4. Hold Meta lead-gen spend flat through Q1 while the geo-targeting leak and CAPI are fixed. This is a low-demand quarter anyway — the right time to fix the funnel before it needs to carry real volume in Q2.
  5. Fund the retargeting build as a Q1 project, not a Q1 media-spend line. The audience rebuild and CAPI restoration are engineering/setup work this quarter; the actual retargeting ad spend can wait until there's a matched audience worth spending against — targeted for a Q1-end/Q2-start soft-launch.

Branding & Retargeting Strategy for Q1

The account currently runs a two-stage funnel: cold Awareness video (reach only, no follow-up) and an immediate Lead-gen ask. Q1's job is to build the missing warm middle stage while demand is naturally low, so it's ready before the Q2 cooling ramp needs it:

  1. Cold — Awareness (existing, keep running as-is). No change to the buy; $2.40 CPM is efficient.
  2. Warm — Retargeting (new, this is the Q1 build). - Rebuild the pixel-based website-visitor custom audience (none exists today). - Stand up a video-viewer custom audience off the Awareness campaign's own delivery — 333K impressions already sitting there unused. - Retire the three stale 2017 lookalike audiences. - Restore CAPI first — without it, a value-based lookalike can't be built this quarter, only basic visitor/viewer audiences. The qualified-lead lookalike becomes a Q2 item once CAPI has been live long enough to accumulate real signal.
  3. Conversion — Lead-gen (existing, fix before scaling). Geo-targeting fix applies in Q1; actual budget scaling waits for Q2 per the allocation section above.

Target for end of Q1: CAPI restored, two clean audiences built (site visitors, video viewers), and — if both land on schedule — a small retargeting campaign soft-launched in March. If either technical fix slips, hold the retargeting launch rather than force the date; this mirrors the same discipline used elsewhere in TSD's book (don't hand a new lever dirty signal).

Opportunities to Improve Qualified Lead Volume & ROI (Q1 Priorities)

  1. Fix the Meta location-targeting leak on both active lead campaigns (22–30% of leads landing outside Texas) — the single highest-priority Q1 item on the Meta side.
  2. Add an Instant Form screening question ("Do you live in [specific DFW counties]?") — a same-week fix, doesn't wait on the full targeting audit.
  3. Restore Conversions API — Q1 technical prerequisite for the retargeting build and for any future qualified-lead signal back to Meta.
  4. Lift Branded Search's daily budget to close the 11.3% budget-lost impression share — cheapest, most certain win available, should be locked in during Q1 regardless of the rest of the plan.
  5. Verify the July branded-search conversion spike before it anchors Q1/Q2 CPA assumptions.
  6. Don't relaunch Nonbranded Search blind in Q1. Needs a real negative-keyword list and confirmed landing page first — gate it to later in the year.
  7. Pursue LSA detailed-lead-reporting API access from Google as a Q1 task — LSA is the account's biggest line item and currently unverifiable on quality (disputed/booked rate unknown).
  8. Lay the groundwork for a CRM/booking feedback loop in Q1 (even if the live sync isn't built until Q2) — once CAPI is restored, disqualified/out-of-area/no-show leads need a path back to Meta so the algorithm stops optimizing toward the cheap-but-unqualified profile.

Evergreen Baseline — What Runs Every Month Regardless of Season

  • Google LSA — the primary lead engine, running year-round; Q1 sees one modest step-up.
  • Google Branded Search — always on; budget lifted to close the impression-share gap.
  • Meta Lead-gen CBO(s) — always on, held flat in Q1 pending the geo-targeting/CAPI fixes.
  • Meta Awareness (ABO video) — always on, feeding the new retargeting layer once it's built.
