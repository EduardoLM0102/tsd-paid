Spencer Air
Q1 2027 Marketing Strategy
Channels: Google Ads, Local Services Ads, Meta Ads, and YouTube Ads
Prepared by TopServ Digital

## Focus for the Quarter

Q1 is naturally the quieter part of the year in this market before cooling demand builds back up in
the spring, so it's the right window to strengthen the foundation the business runs on: keep the
channels that are already working well funded at the right level, and add a brand and retargeting
layer that carries more of the audience already being reached through to a lead. The goal is to head
into the spring ramp with a stronger, more complete funnel rather than just more raw spend.

## Channel Strategy

**Google (LSA and Branded Search) leads the investment and carries lead generation exclusively.**
Local Services Ads has been the strongest performer this year: as investment has increased step by
step, cost per lead has stayed steady in roughly the $75 to $100 range rather than climbing, which
points to more room to grow before diminishing returns set in. A modest, steady increase to the LSA
budget is recommended through Q1.

Branded Search is running efficiently, at a cost per lead in the mid-$30s, and is currently capturing
close to 80% of available branded search volume. A small additional investment picks up more of that
remaining volume at the same low cost, making this one of the easiest opportunities available this
quarter.

**Meta and YouTube together form the awareness and retargeting layer, with no direct lead generation
running on either platform.** Rather than asking for a lead directly through Meta, the role of this
layer shifts to building brand recognition through video across both platforms and following up with
people who've already engaged, so that intent shows up downstream on Google Search and LSA instead.
It's a cleaner division of labor: awareness and warm nurture upstream, direct response downstream.

**Illustrative Q1 monthly investment** across Google, Meta, and YouTube combined, stepping up
gradually across the quarter and landing at roughly **$30,000 to $31,500** for the quarter overall.
Figures are planning estimates for alignment, subject to approval before anything changes.

## Brand and Retargeting Strategy

Meta's video campaign already puts the brand in front of a large local audience, and pairing it with
YouTube Ads extends that same awareness role across another high-attention video platform. Together,
they become a single upper-funnel layer with one job: build recognition and trust before intent shows
up on Google.

This quarter, the retargeting stage gets added: following up with people who've already watched the
videos or visited the website, warming them up ahead of that Google search or call.

Think of it as a three-step conversation instead of two:

1. **Awareness (Meta + YouTube)**: introduce the brand broadly across both platforms through video
   (already running).
2. **Retargeting (Meta)**: a warmer follow-up to people who've already engaged, reinforcing the
   brand and building trust (new this quarter).
3. **Lead generation (Google: LSA and Branded Search)**: the direct ask lives here, now reaching an
   audience that's already familiar with the brand.

By the end of Q1, the goal is to have the retargeting layer live and warming the audience that Google
then converts, setting up stronger, more efficient performance heading into the spring cooling
season.

## Looking Ahead

This quarter is being scoped deliberately rather than locking in a full-year number up front, since
the awareness and retargeting layer is new and still building a track record. Once Q1 results are
in, the Q2 through Q4 view will follow, timed to the demand curve in the area, with the summer
cooling season as the year's biggest investment window.
