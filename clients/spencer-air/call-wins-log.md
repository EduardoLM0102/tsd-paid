# Spencer Air Conditioning and Heating — Call Wins Log

Day-by-day log of every ads-generated, recorded CallRail call for Spencer Air, 2026-10-01 through 2026-10-15 (36 calls). "Ads-generated" here means CallRail tagged the call as Google or Meta ads traffic, or it carries a gclid — this intentionally includes Google Local Services Ads calls, since LSA is this client's main Google engine, not just classic Search/PPC. Recordings were transcribed locally with faster-whisper (phone-quality audio, auto-generated — names/spellings can be off). Nothing below is invented; where a call cuts off or ends ambiguously, that's stated plainly instead of guessing an outcome. No call this period had a customer read out a full card number, expiration, or CVV, so the payment-redaction rule was never triggered.

**Caveat on the "source_name" field:** CallRail's `source_name` is often a generic tracking-pool label ("Homepage Calls") rather than the actual channel. Where that's the case, the real channel (from the `source`/gclid fields) is noted in parentheses so the paid-vs-other picture isn't lost.

**Outcome legend:**
- ✅ Booked — a job got scheduled, or a clear win (membership renewal/upsell, etc.)
- ⏳ Open — real lead, not yet resolved (callback promised, quote requested, voicemail with a real need)
- ❌ Not a lead — wrong number, telemarketer, no real content, or a booked job that got cancelled with no job performed
- ⚠️ IVR-only — call never got past the automated greeting/hold menu; no live conversation audible
