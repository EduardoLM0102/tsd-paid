## Overview

This plan covers the five-month stretch from the tail of Texas cooling season into the heart of heating season. **Google Ads carries the majority of both budget and lead generation**: Search for direct-response demand, YouTube as an awareness layer inside the same platform. **Meta runs lighter and more selectively:** a low always-on baseline for retargeting and brand, concentrated into two seasonal-offer bursts when it's worth asking Meta to generate leads directly. **TikTok remains available as an optional third layer** for awareness only.

August still means broken air conditioners in North Texas. By October the calls shift to furnaces and heat pumps, and by December the conversation shifts again to electrical: holiday lighting circuits, surge protection, and panel capacity for a house full of guests and space heaters. The plan is built to move with that arc rather than run one message for five months.

**Budget note:** This plan is built on channel ratios rather than fixed dollars, so it holds at any spend level. Scenarios below are modeled at $3,500, $5,000 and $7,500 per month.

## What Each Channel Does

- **Google Ads — Search:** Pay per click on active repair, replacement and installation intent: "AC repair," "furnace repair," "electrician near me," "electrical panel upgrade." This is where the homeowner already has a problem and is actively looking for someone to fix it today. Highest-intent channel in the mix, and the majority of both spend and leads in this plan.
- **Google Ads — YouTube (awareness):** In-stream/Discovery video, run inside the same Google Ads account as a smaller awareness carve-out, not a lead-gen line. Puts the brand and the before/after work in front of homeowners in the service area before they're actively searching.
- **Meta (Facebook & Instagram):** Runs lighter than Google overall. Most months it's a low baseline of retargeting and brand awareness, the account's biggest single source of awareness weight even though it spends less than Google. Twice in this window (September, December) it steps up into a direct seasonal-offer push, timed to a specific promotion rather than running as a flat always-on lead channel.
- **TikTok (optional, awareness only):** Short-form video (quick tips, before/after clips, storm-prep content) run on a reach/awareness objective, not lead generation. It feeds top-of-funnel familiarity rather than competing with the other channels for leads.

*Google Ads (Search + YouTube) and Meta are the core of this plan. TikTok is an add-on layer, priced and modeled separately below.*

## Who We're Targeting

- **The Summer Emergency Buyer:** AC is out in a North Texas August, wants a truck today. Owns the home, $100k–$250k household income, no patience for a callback tomorrow.
- **The Comfort Upgrade Buyer:** System is aging or already limping; would rather replace before the first cold snap than gamble on one more season. Financing and clear pricing move this person.
- **The Winter Heating Buyer:** Furnace or heat pump fails (or is about to) as temperatures actually drop in North Texas, a shorter, sharper window than the AC season, so timing the message matters.
- **The Holiday Electrical Buyer:** Hosting for the holidays, worried about tripped breakers, overloaded outlets, or an old panel that can't take the extra load. Panel upgrades, surge protection, and dedicated circuits sell hardest here.
- **The Safety-Conscious Homeowner:** Not in crisis, but has read enough about Texas grid strain to think about backup power and surge protection before the next storm, not during it.

**Logic:** Search carries the plan every month because repair and replacement demand in this category is an active-search event: someone with a dead air conditioner or a failed furnace is typing the problem into Google, not waiting on a Facebook ad. YouTube rides alongside it at a steady, modest share purely for brand familiarity, a smaller, always-on awareness line rather than a seasonal spike. Meta drops to a low retargeting/brand baseline in August, October and November, then steps up twice, in September and December, into a genuine seasonal-offer push, because those are the two moments in the arc where there's an actual promotion worth paying to put in front of people rather than just staying top-of-mind.

**Meta's two offer windows:**

- **September — "Beat the Heat" tune-up/replacement offer.** Direct-response push while AC demand is still live but starting to taper; financing and pre-winter tune-up framing.
- **December — Holiday panel-safety offer.** Direct-response push on panel upgrades and surge protection timed to the holiday-hosting anxiety, TSD's highest-conviction Meta message in this plan.

Outside those two windows, Meta runs as a low-budget always-on baseline (retargeting site visitors and past leads, Ad Recall Lift for brand) rather than being switched off entirely; it stays warm between the two pushes.

**TikTok (optional add-on):** If added, model an incremental **$750–$1,500/month** on top of the totals above, run on a Reach or Video Views objective. Not folded into the core ratios above and not counted toward lead targets; it is a brand and top-of-funnel layer, best added starting in October when the before/after furnace and panel-upgrade content has the strongest visual hook.

## Monthly Messaging

### August — Peak AC Repair

- **Angle:** A North Texas August doesn't negotiate. Neither do we.
- **Message:** When the AC goes out in August, it's not a maybe-tomorrow problem. Same-day service, upfront pricing, no overtime surprise.
- **Channel focus:** Google Search on AC repair and emergency terms; YouTube on brand-awareness pre-roll in the service area; Meta stays at a low retargeting baseline for anyone who clicked but didn't book.

### September — AC Tail & Early Replacement

- **Angle:** One more summer, or a new system before the next one.
- **Message:** If the AC limped through August, it's worth asking whether it makes it through next summer. Financing makes replacing it now easier than another emergency call later.
- **Channel focus:** Google Search shifts toward replacement and financing terms; **Meta's first seasonal-offer push runs this month**: "Beat the Heat" tune-up/replacement offer, direct-response, not just retargeting.

### October — Heating Transition

- **Angle:** Get ahead of the first cold snap, not behind it.
- **Message:** A furnace that's been off since March doesn't announce it's failing. The first cold night does. A tune-up now is cheaper than an emergency call in November.
- **Channel focus:** Google Search on furnace tune-up, heat pump and smart thermostat terms; YouTube share increases slightly for before/after furnace content; Meta back to its low retargeting baseline between offer pushes.

### November — Heating Emergency

- **Angle:** The week the furnace finds out it's actually cold.
- **Message:** First real cold snap of the year is when furnaces that skipped a tune-up fail. If the heat's out, we answer.
- **Channel focus:** Google Search back up on emergency heating terms, tracking the cold-snap search spike; Meta stays at its low baseline, retargeting only; the next offer push isn't until December.

### December — Holiday Electrical

- **Angle:** The panel should worry about the holidays, not you.
- **Message:** Lights, guests, space heaters, and a panel that was sized for 2015. Surge protection and a panel upgrade now mean the breaker doesn't decide when the party's over.
- **Channel focus:** **Meta's second seasonal-offer push runs this month**: the holiday panel-safety offer, its highest-conviction message in this plan; Google Search continues to catch heating emergencies and holiday-electrical search terms; YouTube share peaks alongside it for visual panel-upgrade content.

No Cool Hand-specific Meta data exists yet since this is a net-new build.
- **TikTok (if added):** Measured on CPM, reach and video-completion rate, not cost per lead; it's an awareness layer, not a conversion channel.
