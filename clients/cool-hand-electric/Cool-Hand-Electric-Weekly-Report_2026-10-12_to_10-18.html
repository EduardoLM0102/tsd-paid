Calls and booked jobs

We listened to the recorded calls from the week to see what actually happened on each one. These are the calls that came through our tracking line. Since October 14 the landing pages show the PPC tracking number you assigned, 469-270-1146, so calls from the pages now go straight into your ServiceTitan under PPC and will show up in the booked job numbers there.

  • Booked: 3 jobs. A returning customer with a clogged AC drain pan, scheduled for the next morning, who also renewed a lapsed Cool Crowd membership on the call. A new customer for a breaker box inspection plus a quote for a Tesla charger circuit. A light repair for an existing customer, booked on Monday and moved to Tuesday afternoon.
  • Quote shoppers: 2 callers wanted a price first. One was a dedicated outlet for a garage refrigerator, the other a light fixture replacement. Both said they would call back or check with family.
  • Callback owed: 1 after-hours caller about smoke detector replacement left a name and number with the virtual assistant for the office to call back.
  • Not a fit: 1 caller who was looking for a different company.
  • 3 calls reached the virtual assistant greeting and ended before the caller said anything. Two of them were the same number trying again within minutes.

When a caller reached someone in the office, the conversation went straight to the problem and an appointment time. The three bookings above all came from those calls.

Volume this week

Call volume is lower than the summer weeks, and Electrical is the main source of leads. Two things are behind it. Demand is easing as the weather cools. We also tightened the targeting on October 8 and 9 so ads only show to people physically in your service area and stop showing for searches outside what you do. That cut the number of clicks roughly in half, and the clicks that remain are more likely to be real customers. The share of visitors who contact you has about doubled since the new landing pages went live.
