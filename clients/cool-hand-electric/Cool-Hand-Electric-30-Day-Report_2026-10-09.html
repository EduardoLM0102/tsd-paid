5. Branded vs. Non-Branded

  • Non-Branded (Electrical + HVAC): $3,163.65 spent, 51 leads, $62.03 per lead. These are people searching for a service, like “electrician near me” or “ac repair,” who don’t know Cool Hand yet, so this is where new customers come from. Your ads showed on about 12% of the local searches they were eligible for (Electrical 14%, HVAC 11%). Most of the searches we didn’t show on came down to ad position and Google’s ad quality ratings more than budget, and that’s exactly what the October updates go after.
  • Branded (your business name): $628.90 spent, 15.5 leads, $40.57 per lead, and your ads showed on 79% of searches for your name. People searching for Cool Hand are already looking for you, and this campaign makes sure your ad is what they see first instead of a competitor’s.

6. What We Updated (October 8 and 9)

  • Location targeting: your ads now show only to people who are in, or regularly spend time in, your service area (Arlington, Burleson, Ennis, Fort Worth, Mansfield, Midlothian, Red Oak and Waxahachie).
  • Search refinements: we added 34 searches your ads won’t show for, like commercial and industrial jobs, home improvement and supply store searches, people looking for electrician jobs, solar and appliance repair. Searches for your own name now run through the Branded campaign, with four new keywords added there (cool hand hvac, heating, air and ac).
  • New ads: 16 new ads, one for every Electrical and HVAC ad group, each written around the exact service and city people search for. Google approved all 16 on October 9 and they’re the ads running now.
  • Links under your ads: new sets of links for each campaign (7 for HVAC, 8 for Electrical, 6 for Branded) that take people straight to the matching page on your site, like AC Repair, Panel Upgrades and the $37 Safety Inspection.
  • Ad highlights: refreshed the short lines that show with your ads (Family Owned and Local, Financing Available, Work Guaranteed), and the HVAC ads now mention easy payment plans. HVAC repair searches now land on your emergency HVAC page instead of the homepage.
