Branded is the most efficient campaign on cost per conversion, typical for a campaign built on the business’s own name. Electrical and HVAC are each still capturing a modest share of available local search volume – real headroom if increasing their budgets becomes a priority. Google Ads doesn’t track cost separately by conversion type – the same spend and clicks produce both calls and forms – so Cost/Call and Cost/Form above are each campaign’s total spend divided by that count, not an independently priced figure. Phone calls are the large majority of leads across all three campaigns, which is typical for how people search for electrical and HVAC help; with only 1–2 form fills per campaign in this window, Cost/Form will naturally swing a lot from update to update and is best read as a volume signal rather than a stable price.
