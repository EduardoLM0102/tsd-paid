Signs you need an electrical panel upgrade

If two or more of these sound like your house, your panel is working harder than it was built to. That is a safety problem, not just an annoyance.

469-436-5467
  • Breakers trip again and againEspecially when the AC, dryer and microwave run at the same time.
  • Lights dim or flicker when something big kicks onA sign the panel or service can't keep up with your load.
  • The panel feels warm, buzzes, or shows scorch marksStop here and call. This one does not wait.
  • You still have a fuse box or a 100 amp panelCommon in older North Texas homes that now run far more electronics.
  • You're adding an EV charger, hot tub, or new ACBig new loads usually need more capacity before they can be wired safely.
  • Your panel is a Federal Pacific or ZinscoBrands widely flagged by electricians and inspectors for breakers that may not trip.

Panel upgrade questions, answered straight

How much does a panel upgrade cost in Mansfield?

It depends on the amperage you need, whether the meter base or service line needs work, and how many circuits the new panel feeds. We price it after we see your panel and what you plan to run, and you get options in writing before any work starts. Easy payment plans are available.

How long does it take?

Many residential panel swaps are completed in a day. The full timeline also depends on the utility disconnect and the city inspection, so we give you the real schedule when we quote.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Texas electrical contractor license TECL #31768, BBB A+ rated, and members of the Mansfield Area Chamber of Commerce.

Can you wire my EV charger or hot tub at the same time?

Yes. We install EV chargers and hot tub circuits, and the panel upgrade is often what makes them possible. Plan both together and you avoid paying for the work twice.
