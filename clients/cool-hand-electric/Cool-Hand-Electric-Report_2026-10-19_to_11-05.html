What we changed and why

  • October 20: the call button on your Google ads now rings your ServiceTitan PPC number, 469-270-1146, so those calls land in ServiceTitan under PPC with the rest of your calls.
  • October 30: clicks had gotten pricey, about $13 each, so we changed how Google bids on Electrical and HVAC. Clicks now cost $5.28 on Electrical and $7.43 on HVAC, and we get 2 to 3 times more visits on the same budget.
  • October 30 and November 6: we blocked searches for other companies' names and for things you don't do, like duct cleaning and RV parts, so your budget only goes to people looking for your services.
  • October 30: we added new ads that lead with your 4.9 rating from 420 Google reviews, BBB A+, Voted Best in Ellis County and the $25,000 guarantee. They are getting more clicks than the old ads in all four ad groups, up to 5.0% of people who see them against 3.5% for the old ad.
  • November 6: the cheaper clicks brought Electrical fewer calls, so we moved it back to bidding for leads, where it was getting them at about $60 each. HVAC keeps the new setting one more week, and we review everything again on November 13.

Calls and booked jobs

Since the call button now rings ServiceTitan directly, we no longer hear those calls, but Google still logs each one. Of the 15 calls from the Search ads in this period, 14 were answered, 11 lasted over a minute and several ran 4 to 5 minutes. These are real customers talking to your team.

When calls from the ads still ran through our tracking line, from October 10 to 15, we could listen to every one. In those 6 days, 6 real customers called and 3 of them booked a job on the first call: a returning customer with a clogged AC drain pan who also renewed his Cool Crowd membership, a new customer for a breaker box inspection plus a quote for a Tesla charger circuit, and a light fixture repair. The other 3 asked for a call back.

Local Services Ads keeps its own recordings and messages, and we went through those too:

  • Booked: a homeowner called after hours on October 30 about panel work, got a call back, and had an electrician out on November 3.
  • Price first: 3 people sent a message asking for a price and did not book.
  • Not a fit: a commercial job on October 19 that the office referred to another company.
  • A short call on November 2 with no one on the line. Google did not charge for it.
