Overview

August and September are the restroom half of Lilypad's year. The two business lines run opposite each other: portable restrooms carry May through September, septic carries October through April. The Q4 plan puts spend on the septic side. These two months put it on the side that is selling right now, while buying the install leads that fill the fall dig calendar.

An install runs two to five months from first call to dig once permitting and soil tests are counted, and clean digging closes around October. A lead bought in August is a job dug this fall. The same lead in October digs next spring.

Budget note: Built on channel ratios rather than fixed dollars, so it holds at any spend level.

What Each Channel Does

  • Google Ads (Search / PPC): Two jobs at once. Install, lateral field and aerobic terms to fill the fall dig calendar, plus restroom and trailer rental terms, which are real searches no other channel can intercept.
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram): Restroom and trailer bookings. Events, weddings and job sites get planned weeks or months ahead, which is the buying pattern Meta handles well. No Kansas septic or portable restroom advertisers are running on Meta right now, so the channel is uncontested locally.
  • Google LSA (Sewage system): Holds the baseline at a lower share than Q4. Septic emergencies do not take the summer off and LSA is still the cheapest lead on that demand, but it does not cover restroom rental.

Monthly Messaging

August, Restroom Season and Dig Pipeline. Two things are on the clock: units for the back half of the season, and any new system that needs to be in the ground before the freeze. New tanks as low as $89 a month. Google Ads splits between install terms and rental terms, Meta runs job site and event rentals, LSA holds pumping and repair.

September, State Fair and Pre-Winter Turn. The State Fair fills every lot in Hutchinson. Once it clears, attention turns to the tank before winter does, and a system called in now is dug this fall instead of next spring. Meta and Google Ads go geo-tight around the Fair for rentals and brand, install terms stay live, LSA picks up as pumping demand returns.

KPIs

  • Google LSA cost per lead: $30 – $60. Benchmarked on 888 contractors and 126,650 leads, with smaller markets trending toward the $30 end.
  • Google Ads: CPC $4 – $9, CPL $80 – $150. A planning assumption, not a quoted rate. The real number comes from our own auction data after 30 days.
  • Meta (restroom and trailer lead gen): CPL $30 – $50. Home services benchmark is $41 and the channel is uncontested locally.
  • Combined monthly leads: 35 – 55 qualified at $3,000/month, scaling with budget. Slightly under the Q4 figure because LSA, the cheapest source, runs at a lower share.
  • Response time: under 15 minutes on Meta leads, immediate on LSA and call extensions.
  • Watch metrics: booked-job rate, cost per booked job, and how many fall digs trace back to an August or September lead. Rentals and pumping keep the channels fed. Systems at $3,000 to $15,000 are what make these lead costs pay.
