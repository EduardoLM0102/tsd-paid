## Pre-launch gates

- **LSA eligibility — run ZIP 67401 and 67501 through Google's eligibility tool (ads.google.com/localservices, requires a Google account/signup flow) with "Sewage system" selected.** Sewage system is a live standalone US LSA category (separate from Plumbers and Drain expert), confirmed on Google's own category list and its LSA product page; it has subcategories (Cleaning, Excavation, Inspection, Installation, Locating, Maintenance and Repairs, Pumping, Removal/Demolition) that map directly onto Lilypad's septic work. But Google does not publish category availability by ZIP, so availability in Salina/Hutchinson is **still unverified** — this is an account-flow check, not something confirmable by web search. The entire primary channel recommendation rests on this. Five-minute check once someone is signing in.
  - **Category-level research done 2026-08-13 (Edu asked, Cole's concern re: few categories fitting their trade):** Confirmed. **Drain expert is restricted to California and Florida only** — not usable as a secondary category in Kansas, drop that idea. **There is no LSA category for portable restroom/porta-potty rental at all** — that business line cannot run on LSA regardless of ZIP, matches what the media plan already assumes. **Plumber** exists nationally but is deliberately out of scope (Lilypad isn't a general plumber, and filing under it would dilute the septic-specific Google Guaranteed positioning). Net: **Sewage system is the only fitting category, full stop** — which is exactly why the ZIP check above is the single point of failure for the whole LSA recommendation.
- **Pull the real Google Business Profile rating and review count.** LSA rank is review-weighted. Facebook shows only 10 reviews, which is thin. Unverified on Google. If the count is low, a review push has to run alongside the campaigns or LSA will not rank.
- **Keyword Planner, geo-scoped to Saline and Reno counties, before committing a budget figure.** In a 46k/40k market the binding constraint is search volume, not click price. The real risk is being unable to spend, not overpaying. This number should set the budget, which is why the plan ships as ratios.
- **End-to-end lead flow test before anything goes live.** TSD hard rule. Tracking is a blank slate here so this is a full build, not a verification.

## Structural facts

- **Two markets, not one.** Salina and Hutchinson are 66 driving miles apart (~75 min). Separate campaigns, geo, and tracking numbers. Never average their performance.
- **Two business lines, counter-cyclical.** Septic carries Oct–Apr; portable restrooms carry May–Sept (they service 1,000+ units/month, plus luxury trailers). Budget should rotate between lines, not scale the account up and down. Kansas State Fair is in Hutchinson, ~350k attendees, a September anchor for the restroom line.
- **Not a general plumber.** No water heaters, repiping, fixtures. Septic, drain/sewer, grease traps, portable sanitation. Confirmed against their site.
- **Install lead-gen must run 2–5 months ahead of the dig** (permitting + soil). Advertise installs in Q1 for a summer dig. Clean dig window is roughly late June–October; KDHE Bulletin 4-2 blocks construction on wet soil, which closes spring in wet years on top of the Dec–Feb frozen-ground close.
- **Meta is not a septic lead channel.** 3–5 year demand cycle, alarm-triggered, buyer goes to Google. A $41 Meta lead booking at 10% loses to a $59 LSA lead booking at 45%. Meta earns budget on restrooms, brand, and the Flush Family Plan list.
- **SEO/brand consolidation is handled** (Edu, 2026-07-20): LilyPad unified in Jan 2025 and hutchseptic.com now redirects. Legacy identities were Salina Septic Service and Hutchinson Septic Service. Out of paid's lane, do not raise with the client.
- **Lineage:** Fuller Septic Tank Service (1952) → Randy Leister (Cole's father) → Cole → LilyPad rebrand Jan 2025. 70+ years local, currently underused in messaging.
