## Overview

Q4 is the septic half of Lilypad's year. The two business lines run opposite each other: portable restrooms carry May through September, septic carries October through April. This quarter puts the spend on the septic side and treats restrooms as forward booking only.

October is the last clean dig month before the ground freezes, so installs and lateral fields lead. November is the emergency peak, when holiday household load pushes tanks past what they can take. December is slow for scheduled pumping but not for emergencies, and it is when the 2027 install pipeline gets built, because an install runs two to five months from first call to dig once permitting is counted.

**Budget note:** This plan is built on channel ratios rather than fixed dollars, so it holds at any spend level. Scenarios below are modeled at $2,000, $3,000 and $5,000 per month.

## What Each Channel Does

- **Google LSA (Sewage system):** Pay per lead at the very top of Google with the Google Guaranteed badge. Google runs Sewage system as its own category, so Lilypad qualifies on its actual trade rather than under a plumbing listing. Best channel for pumping, repair and emergency demand, and it sits above the national lead-resale sites that crowd these results.
- **Google Ads (Search / PPC):** Pay per click on what LSA does not cover. New systems, lateral fields, aerobic units, grease traps, drain and sewer work, financing terms, real-estate inspections. This is where the high-ticket jobs come from.
- **Meta (Facebook & Instagram):** Not a septic lead channel, and that is deliberate. A homeowner only thinks about their tank every three to five years, and almost always on the day it backs up. When that happens they pull out their phone and search, they do not go looking through Facebook. So Meta gets the work it is actually good at here: portable restroom and trailer bookings, which people plan weeks or months ahead, brand familiarity in two towns small enough that being the known name matters, and the Flush Family Plan sold to the existing customer list.

*LSA and Google Ads are separate Google products with separate dashboards. Budget and reporting are tracked independently.*

## Who We're Targeting

- **The Emergency Buyer:** Alarm going off or a backup in the yard. Wants a truck today.
- **The Maintenance Owner:** On a three to five year pumping cycle, usually overdue and not tracking it. The Flush Family Plan is built for this person.
- **The Replacement Buyer:** Failing system or a saturated lateral field. Price is the objection, so $89 a month answers it before it gets asked.
- **The Seller and the Realtor:** Needs an inspection and a pump to close. Small volume, high urgency, repeat referral source.
- **The Event and Site Planner:** Weddings, festivals, construction sites. Luxury trailers book six to eighteen months out, which is why this runs now for next summer.

## Monthly Budget & Strategy

**Channel ratios by month:**

| Month | Phase | LSA | Google Ads | Meta | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **October** | Last Dig & Pre-Winter Pump | 40% | **45%** | 15% | Installs, lateral fields, pump before the freeze |
| **November** | Emergency Peak | **55%** | 30% | 15% | Backups, holiday load, 24/7 response |
| **December** | Freeze & Forward Pipeline | 40% | 35% | **25%** | Frozen-line emergencies, 2027 installs, wedding trailers |

Quarter average: **LSA 45% · Google Ads 37% · Meta 18%.**

**Dollar scenarios** (ranges span the three months above):

| Monthly Total | LSA | Google Ads | Meta | Quarter Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $2,000 | $800 – $1,100 | $600 – $900 | $300 – $500 | $6,000 |
| **$3,000** | $1,200 – $1,650 | $900 – $1,350 | $450 – $750 | **$9,000** |
| $5,000 | $2,000 – $2,750 | $1,500 – $2,250 | $750 – $1,250 | $15,000 |

**Logic:** Google Ads leads in October because that is the last month the ground can be worked, and install intent is the most valuable search Lilypad can buy. LSA takes over in November when emergency call volume peaks and pay-per-lead beats pay-per-click on pure repair demand. In December emergencies keep coming as lines freeze, but scheduled work thins out, so Meta share rises to build the pipeline that pays off in spring rather than chasing a market that is not there.

**Meta split:** roughly 80% lead and retargeting, 20% awareness on Ad Recall Lift. The awareness layer is cheap in a market this size and it feeds the retargeting pool.

## Monthly Messaging

### October — Last Clean Dig & Pre-Winter Pump

- **Angle:** Get it in the ground before the ground says no.
- **Message:** Two things stop being easy once it freezes: putting in a new system, and getting the old one pumped. October is the last simple month for both. New tanks as low as $89 a month.
- **Channel focus:** Google Ads on install, lateral field and aerobic terms; LSA on pumping and repair; Meta on the Flush Family Plan.

### November — Emergency Peak

- **Angle:** The week the tank finds out how many people are in the house.
- **Message:** Thanksgiving is the hardest week of the year on a septic system. Extra guests, extra loads, extra everything going down the drain. If it backs up, we answer, 24/7.
- **Channel focus:** LSA and Google Ads on emergency and backup terms, day-parted hard around the holiday week; Meta on click-to-call.

### December — Freeze & Forward Pipeline

- **Angle:** Plan the spring job now.
- **Message:** Frozen lines and failing systems do not wait for spring, and neither does the paperwork. Permits, soil tests and scheduling take two to five months, so a system planned in December is a system dug in June. Booking a spring or summer event, the restroom trailers go fast.
- **Channel focus:** LSA on emergency; Google Ads on install planning and financing; Meta on 2027 event and wedding trailer bookings.

## KPIs

- **Google LSA cost per lead: $30 – $60.** Benchmarked on 888 contractors and 126,650 leads: plumbing $57, drain and sewer $59, with smaller markets trending toward the $30 end.
- **Google Ads: CPC $4 – $9, CPL $80 – $150.** Treat the CPC as a planning assumption rather than a quoted rate. Rural auctions run well below the $10.49 national plumbing median because there are few bidders here, but the real number comes from our own auction data after 30 days.
- **Meta (restroom and trailer lead gen): CPL $30 – $50.** The home services benchmark is $41, and there are currently no Kansas septic or portable restroom advertisers running on Meta, so the channel is uncontested locally.
- **Meta awareness:** estimated ad recall lift, plus reach and frequency.
- **Combined monthly leads: 40 – 60 qualified at $3,000/month**, scaling with budget.
- **Response time:** under 15 minutes on Meta leads, immediate on LSA and call extensions.
- **Watch metrics:** booked-job rate, cost per booked job, and the install and drainfield share of revenue. Pumping at $300 to $600 keeps the channels fed. Systems at $3,000 to $15,000 are what make any of these lead costs worth paying.
