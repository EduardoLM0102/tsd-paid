Measuring return, and where things stand

  • What the ad platform reports. Spend, reach, clicks, cost per click, and the leads it was able to record.
  • What it does not report. Which of those leads turned into booked jobs, and what those jobs were worth. That sits in ServiceTitan.
  • To answer the return question properly we need a revenue by source export from ServiceTitan to line up against this spend.
  • PPC is paused. Nothing has run since July 17 and nothing is scheduled.
  • Attribution work is underway. We are rebuilding conversion measurement, including the ServiceTitan booked job connection, so future results are provable rather than estimated.
  • The next step is yours. That export is the missing half of the calculation and the one piece we cannot pull ourselves.
