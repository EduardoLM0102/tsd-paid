**Why these words**
- "Tune-Ups" instead of "Maintenance": the homeowner's word, and shorter on mobile.
- "Replacement" instead of "Install": most jobs replace an old system. "Installation" is fine if preferred.
- No seasonal line in either subhead, so the cooling page works in October and spring. It also avoids "beat the heat", which is banned by the brand rules.
- Each subhead carries one approved brand line ("Stress Free HVAC", "Your Neighborhood HVAC").
- Every claim comes from the site or Agency Titan. No star rating or review count yet: pull the real numbers from the Google Business Profiles first. There are 2 GBPs (Duluth and Alpharetta), so use the one that matches the page.

## Page structure (from Edu)

1. Sticky top bar: logo, star rating, tap-to-call number.
2. Hero: H1 "[Service] in [City]", one-line subhead, Call button + 3-field form (name, phone, ZIP), rating badge, one real photo.
3. Trust strip: licensed and insured, years in business, review count, 2 to 3 badges.
4. Reviews: 3 to 6 real Google reviews (first name + town).
5. Signs you need [service]: 5 to 6 symptoms or problems.
6. How it works: 3 steps (call or book, tech comes out, job done).
7. Mid CTA: button that jumps to the form + phone.
8. Service area: list of towns + map embed.
9. FAQ: 4 to 5 questions (cost, timing, licensed?, areas).
10. Final CTA: form + phone.
11. Footer: business name, address, license #, hours, privacy policy.
12. Mobile: sticky bottom bar with Call | Get Quote.
