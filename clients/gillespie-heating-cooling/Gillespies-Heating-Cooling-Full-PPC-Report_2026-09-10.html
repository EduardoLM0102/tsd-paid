3. What Changed

Campaign Launch (August 18)

  • Two Search campaigns went live: a Branded campaign that protects your business name in search results, and a Non-Branded campaign built around your core services (AC repair, furnace repair, heat pumps, and general HVAC), covering Northern Kentucky and the Cincinnati metro.
  • Full lead tracking (form submissions) went live alongside the campaigns, so results have been measurable from day one.

Delivery Refinement (Late August)

  • In the campaigns' first week, we concentrated delivery specifically on Google Search results rather than syndicated partner placements, sharpening the accuracy of the reporting going forward.

Budget Expansion (Early September)

  • Budget was increased in early September to capture more of the available local search volume. The impact shows clearly in the Weekly Trend table above: both impressions and clicks climbed meaningfully from September 1 onward, and cost per lead improved to $171.65 for the week of September 1 to 7, the best week on record so far.

4. Branded vs. Non-Branded

  • Non-Branded (core services): carrying effectively all delivery to date, with 87 clicks, 1,696 impressions, and 6 leads.
  • Branded (protecting your business name): minimal volume so far. Brand-name search volume for a business your size is naturally light, and this campaign is still in an early data-gathering phase. We're keeping it running and giving it more time to build.

5. What's Next

  • Sharpen ad group targeting using the real search terms now coming in, concentrating budget on your highest-intent searches.
  • Continue tracking cost per lead weekly as the account moves further past its initial ramp period. The trend so far points in the right direction.
  • Keep the Branded campaign live and revisit its volume in the coming weeks as brand awareness in the service area builds.
