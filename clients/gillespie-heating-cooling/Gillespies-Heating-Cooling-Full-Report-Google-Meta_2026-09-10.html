  • Lead Generation: a single Instant Form ad set covering Northern Kentucky and Cincinnati, homeowners 30 and older, running at $50 a day since July. 4. What's Next

  • Sharpen Google ad group targeting using the real search terms now coming in, concentrating budget on your highest-intent searches.
  • Keep the Branded Google campaign live and revisit its volume in the coming weeks as brand awareness in the service area builds.
  • Continue tracking cost per lead weekly on both channels as the account moves further past its initial ramp period. The trend on Google points in the right direction, and Meta's awareness phase is on track to build the reach it's meant to.
  • Plan the right timing to bring Meta lead generation back online alongside the awareness work, so the two phases reinforce each other instead of running one at a time.
