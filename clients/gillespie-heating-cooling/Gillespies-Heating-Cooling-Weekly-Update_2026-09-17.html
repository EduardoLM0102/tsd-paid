Gillespie's Heating and Cooling

Google Ads Weekly Update: September 11 to September 17, 2026

Channel: Google Search Ads. Prepared by TopServ Digital.

This week's work was on what happens after someone clicks your ad. We looked at exactly what a homeowner sees on their phone when they land on your site, and made changes so the path to your estimate form is as short as it can be. Here's what changed and why.

1. What Changed and Why

Two new emergency landing pages (September 17)

What we did: two new pages went live, one for AC repair and one for furnace repair. The estimate form is on the first screen on a phone, with your phone number visible right at the top. Tracking was checked on both before traffic went to them, and we ran a full test from click to form submission.

Why: someone searching for AC or furnace repair usually needs it soon, and they aren't going to scroll through a long page to find a form. On the pages your ads used to send people to, the form sat several screens down on a phone. Every click costs money, so the form should be the first thing they see.

Ad destinations matched to the search (September 17)

What we did: AC repair and general HVAC searches now go to the AC repair page. Furnace and heat pump searches go to the furnace repair page. Searches for your business name go to your homepage.

Why: the page should match what the person just typed. Someone who searched for AC repair should land on AC repair, not a general page. People searching your business name already know who you are, so the homepage is the right place for them. There's no heat pump page yet, so those searches share the furnace page for now.

Campaigns paused and restarted (September 17)

What we did: we paused both campaigns while the new pages were connected and turned them back on later the same day. Budgets stayed the same, $65 a day on Non-Branded and $1 a day on Branded.

Why: we didn't want to pay for clicks to the old pages while the switch was underway, and we wanted every ad group pointed to a page we'd checked before the ads ran again.

Bidding held steady

What we did: kept bidding as it is, and we'll leave it alone for the next two weeks.

Why: the pages and the restart both changed this week. If we change bidding on top of that, we can't tell what caused any movement in the results. Two clean weeks on the new pages first, then we decide.

2. What's Next

  • Automated bidding. After the two steady weeks, we'll move Non-Branded to automated bidding once there's enough data for it to work with.
  • Search term cleanup. Some of the searches coming in are competitor names and do-it-yourself questions, like what it costs to replace an HVAC system. We'll block those so the budget goes to people looking to hire.
  • Heat pump page. Heat pump searches share the furnace page right now. A dedicated page would match them better.
  • Branded budget. Branded got no impressions this week. At $1 a day and with searches for a local business name being low in volume, it moves slowly. We'll go over the budget with your account manager once the new pages have a track record.
