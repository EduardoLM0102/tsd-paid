Facebook and Instagram Ads Report

Lead generation: August 11 to September 9. Awareness: August 30 to September 28, 2026.

Prepared by TopServ Digital, September 29, 2026

This report covers your Facebook and Instagram ads in two parts. The first is the lead generation campaigns, over the 30 days leading up to September 9, when we paused them. The second is the awareness campaign that has kept running since, over the last 30 days through yesterday. Before the numbers, here's why the account is set up the way it is right now.

1. Why Lead Generation Is on Pause

In early September your team let us know the Facebook leads weren't coming through. When we looked into it together, the leads were arriving. Every form someone fills out on Facebook or Instagram goes straight into your GoHighLevel account with their name and phone number. The part that wasn't carrying over was the phone number in the email notification Hatch sends your team, so the alert showed up without a number to call back.

We didn't want to keep paying for new leads until that handoff between GoHighLevel and Hatch was sorted out, so on September 9 we paused the lead campaigns and shifted Facebook over to awareness. That keeps Mission Accomplished in front of people across Lake Havasu City, Bullhead City, Kingman, Parker and Needles every day, and it doesn't rely on the form at all.

2. Lead Generation Results, August 11 to September 9

The lead campaigns ran to people within 50 miles of Lake Havasu, using Meta's own lead form. They went through three rounds of ads: the early August set, a free second opinion offer in mid August, and a new set of offers that went live August 28, after a short break from August 20 to 27 while it was being put together. Over the 30 days the ads were shown 22,086 times and got 148 link clicks, a 0.67% click rate, and 5 leads came in through the form.

3. Awareness Results, August 30 to September 28

The awareness campaign went live September 3, so the numbers below cover September 3 through September 28. It runs six short videos to adults 30 to 65 within about 25 miles of Lake Havasu City, Bullhead City, Kingman, Parker and Needles.

An awareness campaign has a different job than a lead campaign. It isn't built to get form fills. It's built to put your name in front of as many local people as possible, often enough that when the AC quits or the heat won't come on, Mission Accomplished is the name they already know.

Estimated ad recall lift is Meta's estimate of how many people would remember seeing your ad if asked within two days. A ThruPlay is a video watched to the end or for at least 15 seconds.

With the lead campaigns on pause, we raised the awareness budget in two steps, from about $10 a day to $20 a day on September 15, then to $30 a day on September 21. Daily reach grew right along with it, from about 1,600 accounts a day in the first two weeks to about 4,400 a day over the last week. A little under 6 in 10 impressions ran on Instagram, about 4 in 10 on Facebook, and a small share on Threads.

For context, awareness runs at about $5 per 1,000 impressions, compared to about $58 on the lead campaigns, so each dollar reaches more than ten times as many screens. It won't bring in form fills on its own, but it gets your name in front of a lot more people for the same money.

4. What's Next

  • Test the Hatch notification. Once a test lead goes from the Facebook form into GoHighLevel and lands in your Hatch email with the phone number showing, we'll turn lead generation back on.
  • Lead with the $125 a month offer. When lead ads come back, the new system offer goes first, since it brought in the most leads at the lowest cost, with fresh versions of it running alongside.
  • Keep awareness on. It'll keep running while lead ads are off and after they're back, so the people who've already seen your videos recognize the name when a lead ad shows up.
