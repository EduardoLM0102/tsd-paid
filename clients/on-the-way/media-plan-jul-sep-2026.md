## Overview

This quarter is peak season on the Texas Gulf Coast. Unlike a heating-market plan, there is no "cooling-to-heating" pivot here — AC demand stays at full boil all three months, plumbing runs as a steady base year-round, and electrical climbs into September as hurricane/storm season peaks (generators, surge protection, battery backup). The plan captures all of that high-intent demand while simultaneously building the On The Way brand across three trades, so the name is already familiar the next time a homeowner needs a plumber or electrician — not just an AC tech.

The voice stays plain, local, and trustworthy: a 50-year, family-owned Santa Fe company that does top-quality work and stands behind it. Three trades, one phone call.

Why brandformance, and why now: On The Way has three structural advantages most competitors don't — a half-century local reputation, 1,650+ reviews at 4.8 stars, and three trades under one roof. Conversion ads turn today's searches into booked jobs; the awareness layer compounds that reputation so the cost-per-lead on the conversion side drops over time. Industry data backs this up: businesses running social + search together see cost-per-lead fall by roughly 3–29% versus search alone, because the awareness layer warms the audience and feeds the retargeting pool. None of the brand spend is wasted — it makes the conversion spend cheaper.

## What Each Channel Does

- **Google LSA (Local Services Ads)** — Pay-per-lead, sits at the very top of Google with the verified-business badge. The cheapest, highest-intent channel for emergency AC, plumbing, and electrical — and the top priority to activate this quarter. With a 4.8★/1,650-review profile and three active state licenses, On The Way should rank well quickly.

- **Google Ads (Search / PPC)** — Pay-per-click on the high-value searches LSA doesn't fully cover: AC installs & replacements, mini-splits, panel upgrades, generators, water heaters, financing, plus brand-defense and competitor terms. This is where the $9,000 install jobs come from.

- **Meta (Facebook & Instagram)** — Three roles in one account, on separate campaigns: (1) lead generation (Instant Forms — seasonal offers, tune-ups, financing), (2) retargeting (warm traffic, past callers, site visitors), and (3) awareness / recall lift (the ~15% brand layer). For urgency trades, Meta is demand generation — it reaches the homeowner 30–90 days before they search, then retargeting closes them.

Note: LSA and Google Ads are separate Google products with separate dashboards, billing, and reporting. Track them independently.

## Who We're Targeting

Five buyer personas, mapped to the three trades:

- **Quality-Focused (Do-It-For-Me):** wants craftsmanship, warranties, licensed techs. Skews to installs, panel upgrades, repipes. Lever: 50 years, satisfaction guarantee, licensed (TACLA/TECL/RMP).
- **Time-Poor (Convenience / Speed):** wants it handled today. AC repair, drain clog, no-power calls. Lever: click-to-call, same-day, "we're On The Way."
- **The Researcher (Skeptic):** reads reviews, compares quotes. High-ticket installs & generators. Lever: 4.8★/1,650 reviews, financing transparency, retargeting.
- **The Deal Hunter (Price-Sensitive):** waits for an offer. Tune-ups, maintenance, insulation. Lever: seasonal specials + 0% financing.
- **The Emergency Buyer (Panic):** AC dead in 100° heat, burst pipe, dead panel, no power after a storm. Calls whoever answers first. Lever: LSA + call-only urgency, 24/7 framing.

Audience-build for Meta: homeowners, age ~30–65, owner-occupied, within service radius; layer lookalikes off the customer list and "likely to move" / older-home signals. Retarget all site visitors and past leads.

## Monthly Allocation & Strategy

Shown at the recommended $9,000/month; both buckets scale proportionally with budget. Within each month, the conversion budget is split across LSA, Google Ads, and Meta as demand dictates (see the rotation note below the table).

| Month | Lead-Gen (Conversion) | Awareness / Recall Lift | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| July — Peak Cooling | ~88% (~$7,920) | ~12% (~$1,080) | Emergency AC & same-day repair; plumbing base |
| August — Replacement | ~85% (~$7,650) | ~15% (~$1,350) | AC installs, mini-splits, air quality, financing |
| September — Storm / Pipeline | ~82% (~$7,380) | ~18% (~$1,620) | Generators & electrical, AC tune-ups, fall pipeline |

Channel rotation within the lead-gen budget — fluid by design:

- **July — LSA-led.** Emergency search intent is at its annual peak; pay-per-lead at the top of Google is the most efficient way to catch "AC repair near me" and "burst pipe" calls.
- **August — Google-Ads-led.** The high-ticket window. Install, replacement, mini-split, and financing searches convert into $9k jobs; Meta retargets July callers who didn't book.
- **September — Meta-led.** Build the Q4 pipeline through lead forms and retargeting, while Google Ads shifts toward electrical/generator terms as hurricane season peaks and AC tune-up demand begins.

These are emphases, not fixed line items. Inside the conversion budget, dollars move freely between LSA, Google Ads, and Meta as demand spikes and cost-per-lead shifts week to week. The fixed strategic decision is the conversion-vs-awareness split shown in the table; the channel allocation underneath flexes with the market.

Awareness layer (~10–20%): runs every month on Reach / Video Views with the Ad Recall Lift objective — ≈$1,080 (Jul) → ≈$1,350 (Aug) → ≈$1,620 (Sep). Same brand creative, growing investment, feeding the retargeting pool.

Scaling the budget. $9,000/month is the recommended starting point; a realistic working range for this size and the ~200-lead/month target is ~$6K–$12K/month. Paid carries roughly half to two-thirds of the lead goal — the rest comes from organic, referral, and repeat (a real strength given 50 years and 1,650+ reviews). The fastest lever toward the $3M (from $2.2M) goal is funding the July–August AC peak more heavily, since that's when intent and ticket values are highest.

## Operational Guidelines

Speed of response wins the job. Answer every call promptly — LSA rewards fast pickup and penalizes missed calls in its ranking. Respond to Meta leads within 5–15 minutes; fast follow-up makes a lead up to 21× more likely to convert.

Geo targeting — two tiers. Spend efficiently by tier:
- **Core (lower cost, base demand):** Santa Fe, Hitchcock, Bacliff, La Marque, Dickinson, Alvin, Texas City.
- **Metro edge (higher intent & ticket, pricier clicks):** Clear Lake, League City, Friendswood, Webster, Seabrook, Kemah — cap spend here.

Budget is fluid by design. Shift dollars between LSA, Google Ads, and Meta as demand and cost-per-lead move. On mild days when LSA spend runs low, move the surplus to Meta or Google Ads rather than letting it sit idle — that flexibility is built into the conversion budget.

LSA discipline. Answer every call, dispute bad leads weekly (wrong service type or out of area), and keep service area and job categories tightly configured so budget only goes to leads On The Way can actually serve.

Scheduling. Advertise only the hours the team can answer; day-part repair ads to staffed hours and pause LSA when no tech is on call.

Creative standards. 15-sec vertical Reels (9:16) of real On The Way techs and trucks; 1:1 statics carrying the family-owned identity, the 4.8★/1,650-review proof, and the license badges; no stock photos; warm, jargon-free voice. Lead with reviews and the 1976 heritage — they're the brand's biggest differentiators.

## KPIs

Benchmarks below are 2026 home-services data, segmented by trade and channel. Greater Houston is a competitive metro, so expect the higher end of each range, especially during the summer AC peak.

**Google LSA — cost per lead (pay-per-lead):**
- HVAC/AC: $50–$65 (emergency AC in peak summer can spike to $100+)
- Plumbing: $55–$70
- Electrical: $40–$50 (the cheapest trade on LSA)
- Drain/Sewer: $55–$60 · target book rate 40–45%

**Google Ads (Search) — cost per lead:**
- Branded: ~$30–$40 — run a branded campaign to defend the name; cheap and high-converting given the brand strength.
- Non-branded: $100–$170 (AC-repair terms run highest, ~$200+; plumbing ~$167) — but this is where high-ticket install intent lives.
- Performance Max: ~$70–$90 · CPC reference: repair $20–$40, install $45–$75, electrician $12+
- HVAC has the highest conversion rate of any industry (~6.5%); emergency terms convert far higher.

**Meta — cost per lead:**
- Lead gen: $35–$55 (HVAC/electrical); plumbing trends higher ($60–$75)
- Retargeting: meaningfully cheaper (warm audience)
- Awareness: track estimated ad recall lift, plus reach and frequency — not CPL

**Program-level:**
- Combined qualified leads: ~90–130/month at $9,000 (scales with budget; metro costs trend higher)
- Response time: under 5–15 min for Meta leads; immediate for LSA and call-extension calls (5-min follow-up makes leads up to 21× more likely to convert)
- The metrics that actually matter: booked-job rate, cost-per-booked-job, and ROAS — a $200 AC-repair lead that becomes a $9,000 install is cheaper than a $40 lead that never books. Watch these, not raw CPL.
