## Standing parameters for this batch

- **Shot by a company videographer on location.** Not self-recorded. Scripts are production docs with shot direction, real assets (trucks, uniforms, the actual unit/panel/slab), full-body walk-n-talk, b-roll under VO.
- **45s HARD max.** ~100–115 words of spoken copy, tops.
- **CTA, every script:** "Tap Learn More and talk to us right now." Meta, so no phone number on the end card. Arrow down-left to the button.
- **Voice:** natural and organic, never textbook English. Homeowner's vocabulary (AC quit, breaker keeps tripping, water heater died), not agency register.
- **Two shapes per service** while the owner-on-camera question is open: one owner on camera, one VO over b-roll.

# AC / HVAC

## Script 1 — "Thursday"

**Angle:** the wait, not the breakdown. Highest-intent moment there is.
**On camera:** the owner, full-body walk-n-talk.
**Runtime:** ~42s · 105 words spoken

**Hook (0–3s)** — *Owner walks out of the shop bay in Santa Fe, straight at camera, steadicam pulling back ahead of him. Branded truck visible over his shoulder.*

> "It's a hundred degrees out, your AC quit sometime last night, and the company you called can't get here till Thursday."

**Beat 2** — *He keeps walking, reaches the truck, puts a hand on the door. Camera settles to a medium.*

> "We're out of Santa Fe and we cover the whole county. League City, Friendswood, Texas City, Galveston."

**Beat 3** — *Cut to b-roll under the line: truck rolling down a residential street, morning light. Quick cut to a tech stepping down from the cab with a bag.*

> "Get hold of us during the day, you're usually talking to somebody inside the hour. Most days we're at your house that same afternoon."

**Beat 4** — *B-roll continues: night exterior, porch light on, truck pulling up in the dark. Tech at the condenser unit with a work light.*

> "And if it's two in the morning and the house is already at ninety, somebody answers then too. That's what the twenty-four seven actually means."

**Beat 5** — *Back to the owner, now leaning on the truck, closer framing. Direct to camera.*

> "Your AC didn't wait for business hours. We don't either. Tap Learn More and talk to us right now."

**On-screen text:** `Santa Fe, TX` · `Same day, most days` · `24/7 emergency`
**End card:** arrow pointing down-left to the Learn More button. No phone number.

**Production notes:** the night footage is what sells this, so it's worth the second setup rather than faking dusk. The two in the morning line has to land over a real dark exterior or it reads as a claim instead of a fact. Owner should say it walking, not standing still, and the steadicam pull-back is what keeps 42 seconds from feeling like a talking head.
