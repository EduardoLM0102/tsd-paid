READ THIS BEFORE BUILDING
Frequency is measured against 19,493 adults in the fence. It is not the frequency number the platform reports against the audience it actually reached.
A campaign reaching 30% of the fence at 3.0x among those reached is sitting at 0.90x on the fence. That is the misread this sheet exists to stop.
Report fence frequency weekly: weekly impressions divided by 19,493.

DO NOT NARROW THE AUDIENCE
No demographic layering, no interest targeting, no lookalikes on the brand campaigns. The fence is the targeting.
Every exclusion raises effective CPM and shrinks the fence we are paying to hold at 3.0x. Windows are a long-cycle replacement purchase and the person who hears the name today is often not the buyer, they are the one who recommends the buyer in eighteen months.
Conversion campaigns may use retargeting. Brand campaigns may not.

BUYING RULES
Meta and YouTube run as reach and frequency or impression-based buys, not cost per click. We are buying impressions against a fixed population.
Set frequency caps to protect 3.0x, not to chase higher. 4.0x costs 22% of the fence at this budget.
Branded search is defensive only. No unbranded keyword spend comes out of this budget.
No offers, no coupons, no discount creative in the brand campaigns.

FATIGUE IS THE KNOWN FAILURE HERE
Every asset so far has been shot and cut the same way. On an impression buy at 3.0x a week, sameness burns out fast.
Do not launch with fewer than 4 distinct executions in rotation.
Flag creative fatigue by week 6 and rotate, do not wait for performance to fall.
