2. Q4 2026 So Far

October came to $4,672 across all platforms. November 1 to 12 is at $2,011, which puts the month on pace for about $5,000. Q4 is running below Q3 for two reasons: Local Services Ads only charges per lead, and the summer peak in July and August (about $2,850 a month) eased off from September as glass and window calls cooled with the season. Facebook and Instagram lead ads also ran at a reduced level from mid-October until November 10, while the ad set was rebuilt.

4. One Seasonal Note

Local Services leads dip in November and December and come back in January. Last winter you had 18 leads in November, 12 in December, 33 in January, 25 in February and 50 in March. Since that channel only charges when a lead comes in, December will land a little light on that line and the spend shows up again from January, when the calls do.
