Ryan's rule: surplus is a deployment decision (widen the fence, add YouTube, move to capture), not a lower invoice. Decide that BEFORE launch, not after the cap bites.
2. **Branded Search cannot spend $640/mo.** It spent $39-75 a month on 50-95 searches at 87-97% impression share. Until brand search grows, ~$100/mo is the realistic ceiling; the other ~$540 has no home in Search.
