# TopServ (TSD) — Own Meta Ad Copy Log

TSD's own lead-gen ads. Buyer = home service business owners (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing) doing **$1M+/year**, often spending ~$10K/month on marketing without growth to match. Offer = "book a call with our team." Videos are VSLs; the primary text + headline just wrap them.

Schwartz read: **product-aware prospect, stage-4 market** (they've hired agencies, heard every "we'll rank you" pitch, skeptical). Lead with the mechanism (the "half you're missing" / Brandformance / video-as-infrastructure), qualify hard on revenue + spend, let the video carry the story. Voice = owner-to-owner, no jargon, zero AI tells, no em dashes.

---

## COPY FORMAT RULES (follow every time — these are Edu's corrections, do not repeat the mistakes)

1. **One shared set per video batch.** Deliver **2 Primary Texts + 2 Headlines TOTAL** that wrap ALL the videos in the batch — NOT one set per video. So the copy must be generic to the offer/mechanism, **no client names or per-video story details** in it.
2. **Two sizes: one smaller, one medium.** Primary Text 1 = smaller, Primary Text 2 = medium. Same for headlines (1 smaller, 1 medium). Keep both TIGHT — Edu's bar for "medium" is short; if it looks long, it's too long.
3. **First line (hook/header) = 9 words MAX** so it's fully visible on mobile before the "See more" cut.
4. **Keep paragraph spacing** — blank line between paragraphs. (He wants the whitespace.)
5. **A couple of emojis** — tasteful, ~1 per paragraph, not per sentence.
6. Headline field = short and punchy.
7. Voice rules (TSD client-facing): natural/organic, homeowner-owner register, no em dashes, no "not just X, it's Y", no empty adjectives, no textbook English.
