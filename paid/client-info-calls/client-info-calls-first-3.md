## Spencer Air Conditioning and Heating

### Differs from what's on file

- **24/7 and Locations (hours).** The hours on file (every day 00:00 to 12:00) look like a typo for 24 hours. Calls show live agents answering at 7:30 pm, 11 pm and 2:19 am and booking "standby" emergency visits. Emergency service isn't guaranteed, though: on Oct 13 the agent said no tech was free that night, and at 2:19 am the customer was still waiting because the on-call tech hadn't answered. Suggest "phones answered 24/7, emergency visits when a tech is free" and confirm with them.
- **Where do you work?** Calls Oct 1 to 16 came from well past the 16 cities on file: Fort Worth, Hurst, Arlington, Rowlett, Cedar Hill, Colleyville, Frisco, Trophy Club, and Prosper (the agent said yes, they service Prosper). Ask if the west side (Fort Worth, Hurst, Arlington) and Frisco/Prosper belong on the site.
- **Business customers.** Yes, with a limit. They fixed a rooftop unit leak for an existing commercial client (Carrollton), reconnected 3 outdoor units for a property manager (Dallas), and got a quote request for a commercial building (Arlington). But on Oct 2 their agent turned down a 90+ unit apartment property, saying Spencer is too small for it. So small and mid-size commercial yes, large multifamily no.
- **Ductless / "winter only" note.** On Oct 13 a condo owner wanted plenum work done now "rather than wait for Spencer's usual cooler-weather window". They also scoped a ductwork and register move in early October. This points to "we do it, but schedule it in cooler months", not "never market it". Still needs their answer.

### Adds

- **Prices.** Agents quote an **$89 trip fee** for a normal diagnostic visit (plus repairs), and **$150** for standby, same-day after-hours and weekend visits ("doesn't apply to the repair cost"). Heard on calls Oct 3, 5, 9, 11, 13, 14 and 15. One customer pushed back on a second $89 for a repeat visit; the agent noted the tech could waive it.
- **Maintenance plan.** Price heard on an Oct 16 call: annual plan renewed for **$348 for two units**, includes checking the heaters. Plan name not mentioned.
- **Who answers the phone.** Live agents, with a hold queue ("please hold for the next available agent, press zero for a callback"). 4 of 36 ad calls (Oct 1 to 15) only show the hold message in the recording.
- **Financing (payments).** Calls show card, company check, check at the visit, and payment links sent by email. Nothing about a financing company.
- **Services.** What callers booked: AC not cooling, condensate and drain lines backing up, thermostat repairs, water leaking from vents, rooftop unit leaks, heater checks, ductwork and register changes, plenum replacement, and full system replacement quotes (4-ton). Replacement quotes go to "management will call you back with pricing".
- **Jobs by city** (partial, booked ad calls Oct 1 to 16): Carrollton (AC diagnostic, commercial rooftop leak), Dallas (outdoor units reconnect), Hurst (AC out, after hours), Rowlett (condensate line), Coppell (thermostat, duct and register move quote), Colleyville (unit leaking water), Irving (vent leak diagnostic), Frisco (two heaters plus plan renewal), Grand Prairie (thermostat), Trophy Club (commercial studio leak).

- **Locations (phone).** The site uses CallRail number swapping (company 526120685), so visitors see a tracking number, not always (972) 446-2665. The new site needs the swap script kept.
- **Second address (3006 Skyway Cir S).** Nothing in our logs.

## Proud Pops Plumbing, Air and Electric

### Differs from what's on file

- **Hiring** (on file: no). The site has a live careers page (proudpops.com/careers) with a job application form. It got about 130 applications from Sep 7 to Oct 6, about 100 of them from Google Ads. Either they're hiring or the page should come down. Ask which roles.
- **Year founded / earlier company.** Proud Pops is a rebrand of The Problem Solvers (txproblemsolvers.com). The site switched to Proud Pops between Mar 16 and 27, 2026, and the Google Ads history runs unbroken from Jan 2025. My Favorite Service Company (owner Jacob) merged in. The site (Oct 8) says family owned by Jacob and Brandon Rackler, and our branded ads say "The Problem Solvers is now Proud Pops". So the business is older than 2025/2026. Ask which year and which earlier names they want shown.
- **Where do you work?** Their own LSA service area lists 47 to 50 cities. The 12 on file miss New Braunfels and most of the northeast side: Universal City, Selma, Schertz, Cibolo, Garden Ridge, Timberwood Park. Also missing inner suburbs: Alamo Heights, Castle Hills, Leon Valley, Kirby, Windcrest, Live Oak. Farther LSA towns: Kerrville, Pleasanton, Hondo, San Marcos.
- **Locations.** New Braunfels looks like a second location: AT already has a New Braunfels Business Profile, we run a YouTube campaign there, and the ads say "Serving San Antonio and New Braunfels". There are also 3 separate LSA profiles. (210) 225-5767 is the number on the Google call extension. The hours on file (Mon 00:00 to 12:00, other days 9 to 5) look wrong.
- **24/7.** Our plumbing ads (written from the site Oct 8) say 24/7 emergency plumbing. Same-day appointments are claimed for HVAC only, not plumbing or electrical.
