Why it keeps happening, and the fix

Every Agency Titan video process ends with the AM sending files to the client. There is no step that gives the finished files to Paid. On Nick's, Jessica asked in the task on Sep 11 "does the SEO and Ad department know?" and nobody answered. That is the LilyPad, Zen Air and King's story.

  1. Add a last step to every video process: "Deliver to Paid", assigned to Paid, with the Drive link and approval status. It closes only when the video is live in the ad account.
  2. Paid this week: Zen Air and King's Window live as soon as approval is confirmed. Nick's swapped to approved cuts. Morrow and Concrete swapped as edits land. Mission Accomplished testimonials into lead ads when the card works.
  3. Move video into lead ads, not only awareness. Watt's Right shows it works.
  4. Decisions needed from you: an editor for Neon Monkey's raw footage (the client is holding Facebook off until it exists), whether Proud Pops gets new video back in the program, and a refresh for All Seasons / C&S.
