Observations

Zen Air Heating and Cooling dominates this list: "ac technicians," "a c technician," "air conditioner technician," "ac technician," and "hvac technicians" together account for roughly 420 of the book's 1,009 non-branded search-term conversions, at very low cost (under $1/click on the top term). This is the same pattern flagged in the companion keyword report — verify these against GA4/CRM before treating them as verified leads; a job-seeker or curiosity search ("hvac technician" as a career query) converting on a lead form at this rate would be unusual and is worth ruling out as a tracking/conversion-action issue.

All Seasons AC's converting non-branded queries are still largely competitor and distributor names even after excluding the account's own brand and manufacturer terms: "petro home services," "gallettair," "weber and grahn southampton," "meenan oil company," "burts reliable southold." These are real competitor/distributor names in its Long Island / NY market picked up by broad match. Some convert well and may be intentional competitor-conquest terms; any not intentional are candidates for negatives.

Proud Pops, Golden Plumbing, and All Heart show a mix of generic local-intent terms ("home services near me," "plumbing contractors in my area") and a couple of competitor names ("roto rooter dishwasher," "tom drexler plumbing") converting at meaningful volume — also worth a quick negative-keyword review if unintentional.

C&S Air, Gillespie's Heating and Cooling, and Live Wire Electric show zero non-branded search-term conversions in this window, consistent with paused, new, or rank/budget-constrained non-branded campaigns on those accounts.

Note: search terms are the raw text a user typed before Google matched it to a keyword, so intent can differ from the keyword that triggered the ad. Branded exclusion = campaign/ad-group name containing "brand" (excluding explicit "nonbranded"/"unbranded" labels) OR query text matching the account's own company name or carried manufacturer brand. Figures aggregate the same query text across accounts where more than one client picked it up.
