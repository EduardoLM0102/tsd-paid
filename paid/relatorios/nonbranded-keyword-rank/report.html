Observations

Zen Air's "local hvac repair company" (264 conv, 5,153 clicks) and "hvac contractors near me" (250 conv, 4,771 clicks) are unusually large relative to everything else in the book — both from the recently built TSD_08.03_Search_NonBranded_Evergreen campaign. The blended cost per conversion here is under $6, far cheaper than any other account's non-branded terms. Given other accounts in this book (All Seasons AC, Proud Pops) have documented histories of conversion actions counting clicks/page-views rather than verified leads, this pair is worth a direct GA4/CRM cross-check before treating it as 514 real leads.

Outside of Zen Air, Neon Monkey Services (tree/stump work) and Watt's Right Electric (EV charger/electrician) show the next-strongest, most plausible non-branded volume, both with clean single-account keyword sets and reasonable CPAs.

Only 13 of 15 active accounts produced non-branded keyword conversions in this window; C&S Air and Mission Accomplished show zero, consistent with paused or rank/budget-constrained non-branded campaigns noted on those accounts.

Note: figures aggregate keyword text across accounts — where multiple clients target the same generic term, conversions are the sum across those accounts.
